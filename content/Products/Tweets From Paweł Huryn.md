- In the last 7 months, 12,000+ PMs completed my skill assessments.
  I identified the key knowledge areas for PMs:
  - Product Owner \ APM
  - Product Manager
  - Senior Product Manager
  - Product Marketing Manager
  - Growth Product Manager
  - Head of Product \ CPO
  There are 8 knowledge areas:
  1. Strategy and Business: Vision, strategy, strategic models, market research, business models, and product portfolio management.
  2. Execution: Working with goals (e.g., OKRs), managing outcomes, roadmaps, Agile, and Lean.
  3. People: Building alignment, leadership (leading with the context and culture), and management (coaching, mentoring, hiring).
  4. Data Analytics: Product analytics, frameworks, AARRR metrics, KPIs, and other techniques (funnel analysis, heatmaps) and data analysis tools.
  5. Product Discovery: Customer research (interviews, surveys), initial product discovery, continuous product discovery, and essential techniques (Kano model, Opportunity Score, Opportunity Solution Tree, and JTBD).
  6. Experimentation: Formulating hypotheses, verifying market engagement, working with user prototypes and spikes, A/B testing, and multivariate testing. 
  7. Product Marketing: GTM strategy (e.g., segmentation, positioning, pricing, channels, customer engagement), enabling sales and partners, and planning and executing marketing campaigns.
  8. Product Growth: Product-led growth, optimizing the entire AARRR funnel, pricing models, and leveraging virality and network effects.
