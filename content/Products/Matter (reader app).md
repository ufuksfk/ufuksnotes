https://hq.getmatter.com/,

If you need to highlight Turkish podcasts while listening, that is only app I found supports turkish language. I am using it instead of [[Snipd]]. Compared to snipd, it is just pure reader app and you can check your podcasts and everything with web browser. Unfortunately, you can't do that with Snipd, and that is very annoying.
