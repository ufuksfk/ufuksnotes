I am reading about it regularly, for the latest [[2025-03#21]]:

https://www.quora.com/Is-it-bad-practice-to-publish-the-same-content-piece-on-LinkedIn-and-on-a-personal-blog-site:
Post it first on your website .
Then the following day or few days later, you can share it on not only LinkedIn, but I’d also consider other platforms like Medium.
What works well is if it’s a 2000 word post on your site, scale it down to 500 words (high-level) on other publishing platforms and then link back to your site to read the post in it’s entirety. Source it back to your blog with text like “This post first appeared on…”
One thing you should also do is use a different headline.
If your just starting out and don’t have a lot of eyeballs on your blog, this can be a way to get more traffic.
With that said, sites like medium take some work and personally I’d rather focus that time on my own blog to reap the long-term benefits.
If your doing this because you lack sufficient traffic yet:
1. Boost your post on Facebook to a competitors (someone in your niche) audience
2. Spend more time reaching out to get exposure and links to this content
3. Share the content in relevant groups, possibly Reddit, which in the right subs can do wonders
4. Collaborate with other content creators with similar audiences and find a way to work with one another.
These efforts and others will reap you further ROI than posting on LinkedIn IMO.

Due to higher site authority, there’s a good possibility that your article on LinkedIn, Medium, or other sites could outrank the original piece on your website in search results.

I’m going to share a secret that many people in my industry aren’t going to like or may find shocking. I post stuff all the time on various platforms. I always make sure it’s on my primary blog first and has been up for 30 days to be indexed.
