[[(LFP) Lithium Iron Phosphate]] vs [[(NMC) Lithium nickel manganese cobalt oxides]]
* They can be used even in the same model of the same car brand.


| LFP                                   | NMC                                       |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
|                                       | better energy density                     |
| safer in heat management              | needs very good thermal management        |
| technology improves for fast charging | historically considered to charge faster. |
| higher battery cycles around 2 - 7k   | lower battery cycles like 1 - 4k          |
| it is good for public vehicles.       | it works better at cold temperatures      |
| safer, more environmental friendly    | it has cobalt, which is rare mineral.     |
