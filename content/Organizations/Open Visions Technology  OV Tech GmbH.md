An open-source SmartWatch requires an open-source SiP. The images below show the PCB used in our Linux/Android capable system-in-package. This first version supports 2GB LPDDR4x memory and 32GB storage.  
  
The design consists of a 10-layer ELIC stackup with relatively common 75um line width/spacing requirements and 85um laser VIAs. We can use these design rules (which are commonly supported by standard PCB manufacturing processes) as we will be using Thintronics substrate, which allows us to achieve our target impedance even with relatively wide traces thanks to its ultra-low Dk. The stackup is based on a very thin film of only 35um thickness. Such a low thickness results in low impedance even at 75um linewidth with standard Dk values. By using an ultra-low Dk substrate, we can take advantage of low-cost PCB manufacturing processes that make this approach feasible even at lower volumes.  
  
I will be presenting more details of the design at the PEDC - Pan-European Electronics Design Conference in Vienna next week.
