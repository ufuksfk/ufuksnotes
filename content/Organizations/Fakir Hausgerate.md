### Notes from German [Wikipedia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fakir_Hausger%C3%A4te):

In the [fire disaster on the Kaprun 2 glacier cable car](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandkatastrophe_der_Gletscherbahn_Kaprun_2 "fire disaster at the Kaprun 2 glacier cable car") on November 11, 2000, which claimed 155 lives, a manipulated, negligently installed household fan heater from the _Fakir Hobby TLB model series (production discontinued in 1996) started the fire in the_ _Kitzsteingams_ train set traveling to the mountain station . The device was intended by Fakir exclusively for household use and, according to [the operating instructions,](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betriebsanleitung "operating instructions") was expressly not designed for use in vehicles
