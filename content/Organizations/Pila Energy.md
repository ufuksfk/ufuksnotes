https://pilaenergy.com/

I listened to these podcasts about Pila Energy and really liked what they are doing:
1. https://www.dertaskforce.com/p/going-permissionless-with-cole-ashmanceo
2. https://cleantechies.substack.com/p/253-pila-cole-ashman-home-electrification

They are trying to distribute energy storage of clean energy into our living space. They said instead of having a full installation battery system, those small modular solution would cut installation cost and it is big cost.

To be able to organize all batteries, they focused [[(IoT) Internet of things]].
Of course their batteries are more expensive in terms of $ per kWh.
# Quick Facts
They are using [[(LFP) Lithium Iron Phosphate]] batteries.
They have very informative and easy to understand website from design details to the keywords.
Their product is really cool! With screen it can be personalised to fit your home decoration.
They also designed plug-in solar panel.
