under [[IEC 60601]], it stands for protection against electrical shock. For patien protection, standard usually requires 2 independent layers, which is referred 2xMOP. It can be 2 basic insulation or one layer of reinforced insulation.
Similarly signal interfaces may need optical or transformer isolation
