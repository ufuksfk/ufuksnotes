It is measures using spesific test setups defined in [[IEC 60601]]
1. Earth leakage
2. Touch current
3. Patient leakage current
For BF(Body floating) devices, patient current must be below 100uA for normal condition and 500uA for single fault
For CF (Cardiac floating) devices, limits are 10uA and 50uA.
