Myth: FR4 is good enough for all RF projects [[High Speed Signals]]

Reality: FR4 suffers from significant dielectric loss at higher frequencies. Combined with inconsistent dielectric constant, it becomes virtually unusable for applications above 1 GHz.[^1]
