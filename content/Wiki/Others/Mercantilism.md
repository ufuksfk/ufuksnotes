Wealth = power = gold
It was dominant economic theory in [[Europe]] from [[16th century]] to [[18th century]].
* Export more than import
* Protect domestic industries
* Colonize territories for cheap raw material
* Control trade routes and suppress competition

How it shows up today?
* Economic nationalism, protectionism
* Strategic industry control
* Foreign exchange reserves
* Export-driven economies
