## [EU Critical Raw Minerals](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/areas-specific-interest/critical-raw-materials/critical-raw-materials-act_en)
In the [[(EU) European Union]], a list of 34 CRMs was defined in the Critical Raw Materials Act. The list is updated every 3 years. The EU’s CRM list published in 2023: 

Aluminium/bauxite 
Antimony 
Arsenic 
Baryte 
Beryllium 
Bismuth 
Borate 
Cobalt 
Coking coal 
Copper* 
Feldspar 
Fluorspar 
Gallium 
Germanium 
Hafnium 
Heavy Rare Earth Elements (i.e., dysprosium, erbium, europium, gadolinium, holmium, lutetium, terbium, thulium, ytterbium, yttrium) 
Helium 
Lithium 
Light Rare Earth Elements (i.e., cerium, lanthanum, neodymium, praseodymium and samarium) 
Magnesium 
Manganese 
Natural graphite 
Nickel* 
Niobium 
Phosphate rock 
Phosphorus 
Platinum Group Metal (i.e., iridium, palladium, platinum, rhodium, ruthenium) 
Scandium 
Silicon metal 
Strontium 
Tantalum 
Titanium metal 
Tungsten 
Vanadium
