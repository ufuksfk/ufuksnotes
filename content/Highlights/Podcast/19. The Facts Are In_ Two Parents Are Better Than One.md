#### ✨ Summary
In a conversation with a fatherhood program director, the question of whether black dads in urban areas can still be involved in their children's lives despite not having stable employment or earnings is raised. The director confirms that they can and expresses that all dads want to be involved. However, due to background charges such as drug or gun charges, these dads are prohibited from attending school events. This highlights the consequences of single-parent households and is further examined by Carney in his research on the declining marriage rate.
