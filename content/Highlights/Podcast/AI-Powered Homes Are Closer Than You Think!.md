# Note
 The house of the future is closer than you think! We're going to need a lot more electricity and water to house the growing population, but newer technologies can help us be more efficient. For example, fridges today use 25% less electricity than they did in the 1970s, and triple-pane windows are 20% more efficient than double-pane windows.

AI can play a big role in making our homes more efficient. It can manage energy, climate control, and even optimize our use of water and electricity. AI can also help us save money by anticipating our needs and making our homes more comfortable.

3D printed houses are another exciting development. They can be built with sustainable materials like hempcrete, bioplastics, and carbon-negative concrete. 3D printing can also be used to create the infrastructure of a home, including piping and electricals. 3D printed homes are also significantly cheaper than traditionally built homes.
