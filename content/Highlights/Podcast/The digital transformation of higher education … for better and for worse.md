## Highlights
* [[2025-03#22]]  I’m joined by Katerina Tomter from the University of AGDA and also one of the editors of a great new open access book, Digital Transformations in Nordic Higher Education. Katharina has been in this field for 30 years and has a really sober perspective on the ways in which digital technologies have actually changed higher education and how this is often not a good thing.
