## Episode AI notes
1. The goal of work is not just to achieve specific metrics or goals, but to actively search for meaningful work.
2. Finding deep meaning in work can be challenging and elusive, but can be found in smaller, intermittent moments.
3. A three-month sabbatical is attainable and companies are open to the idea, providing space for self-reconnection and exploration.
4. Taking a sabbatical is highly recommended in your 30s or 40s, and it takes around 6 to 8 weeks to unwind initially, and another 2 to 3 years for a deeper unwinding.
5. Reaching out to experts and seeking guidance from those ahead on a similar path can provide valuable insight and help.
6. Naming and investing in personal development, including financial agreements, is like investing in your own life MBA.
7. Finding a fulfilling job often involves trying new things, experimenting, and learning along the way.
8. Recommended books on designing life around work are 'The Great Work of Your Life' and 'Wanting'.
