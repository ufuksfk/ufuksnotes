#### ✨ Summary
Counting in human history initially relied on simple methods such as 'one, two, three, many' or counting on body parts like fingers. The use of base 10 for counting is attributed to having 10 fingers, while other societies used different bases based on their body parts. Trade purposes led to the development of natural numbers above one. The concept of zero posed a challenge as it was difficult to understand and represent. The idea of 'zero' was not seen as necessary since having zero of something did not require specific consideration.
