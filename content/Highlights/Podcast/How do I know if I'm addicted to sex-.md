## Highlights
* [[2025-07]]  Hypersexuality, overactive libido and sex addiction are just a few of many terms used to describe those who simply can't seem to get enough when it comes to sex. According to ukrehab.com, it's estimated that somewhere between 3 and 6% of adults in the country are addicted to sex. It might be a hot topic these days, but the concept only really surfaced in the late 1970s.

* [[2025-06]]  For example, this behavioral problem could manifest itself through compulsive masturbation, seeking sex with multiple partners, or excessive viewing of pornography.

* [[2025-07]]  t's important to note that not all mental health experts recognise sex addiction as a formal diagnosis. For example, it was excluded from the 5th edition of the USA's Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders. However, the World Health Organization has its own diagnostic manual called the International Classification of Diseases, and in its most recent edition, Compulsive Sexual Behavior Disorder is listed as a formal diagnosis under the category of impulse control Disorders. You can also find sex addiction tests online, usually in the form of questionnaires.
