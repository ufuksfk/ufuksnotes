# Note
 Vuvitamin is a personalized vitamin company that uses technology to customize vitamin routines based on individual needs, founded by two doctors in 2013.
Vuvitamin has been recognized by various publications as one of the top personalized vitamin subscriptions, offering affordable, all-in-one multivitamins.
The personalized vitamin subscription market is small but growing rapidly, with Vuvitamin leading the way in providing targeted, potent products that reduce pill load and improve efficacy.
Vuvitamin aims to educate consumers and employers about evidence-based nutrition and the importance of personalized supplementation, with a focus on efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and data-driven solutions.
