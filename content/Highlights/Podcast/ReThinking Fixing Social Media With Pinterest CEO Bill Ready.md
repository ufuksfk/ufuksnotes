- **Growth Isn't a Strategy**
  - Don't focus solely on growth; it's not a strategy. Remember that users are real people, not just numbers.
  - Always consider whether you could explain your decisions to them face-to-face and ensure accountability.
  Transcript:
  Bill Ready
  Think growth as a strategy. You know, growth is not a strategy. I mean, I think that's been pretty pervasive in Silicon Valley, growth at all costs. And I think in Silicon Valley, where you have all these platforms with hundreds of millions or billions of users, the customer has become sort of nameless and faceless. And so you get these dystopian-sounding conversations, like harvesting value from users, when it's like, wait a minute, these are real people. These are human beings. If they were standing here in front of you, could you look them in the eye and make that case? I grew up in a little town in Kentucky, my parents had a little auto repair shop. And when I worked in that shop with them, like, I had to go face the customers. And if I messed up, I had to explain to them what I did and how I was going to make it right. And so I try to gauge my decisions on like, well, I still see all those users looking back and we've got 570 million of them, so I don't get to actually talk to all of them. But if I was looking them in the eye, could I explain what I did and why I thought it was right? And I think that's missing. And I think it's a way better strategy than growth at all costs.
