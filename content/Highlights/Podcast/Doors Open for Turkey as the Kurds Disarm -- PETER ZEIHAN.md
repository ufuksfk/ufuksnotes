# Note
 The Kurds, who have been fighting for independence from Turkey for decades, are now in a weakened position. This is due to a number of factors, including the decline of the PKK, the American withdrawal from the region, and the increasing unity of Turkey under Erdogan. This presents a unique opportunity for Turkey to finally resolve the [[Kurdish]] issue.

The [[PKK]], led by [[Abdullah Öcalan]], has called for its members to disarm and become a political party. This is likely due to Ocalan's desire to be released from prison. The Kurds in Iraq and Syria are also facing difficulties, as the Americans have withdrawn from both countries and the Syrian Kurds are being excluded from the new government.

Turkey is now in a strong position to deal with the Kurdish issue. The country is politically unified, economically powerful, and militarily strong. With the Kurds weakened and Turkey's borders secure, the Turkish government can focus on its other strategic goals.
