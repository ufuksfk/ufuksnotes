# Note
 This Therapy Chat podcast episode replays a conversation with Dr. Lindsay Gibson, author of Self Care for Adult Children of Emotionally Immature Parents. Dr. Gibson, a clinical psychologist with 35 years of experience, explains that emotionally immature adults often have underdeveloped emotional skills, leading to inconsistent and confusing behavior. This impacts their children, causing confusion and self-doubt.

The impact of emotionally immature parents is often unrecognized as trauma, but it significantly affects the development of their children. Dr. Gibson emphasizes the importance of recognizing this impact and finding ways to heal and rediscover one's authentic self. Her work focuses on helping people understand the dynamics of these relationships and develop strategies for self-care and boundary setting.

Dr. Gibson's new book offers short, accessible insights into self-care, managing relationships, and coping with challenges. It aims to help readers reconnect with their inner world and trust their instincts, counteracting the effects of being raised by emotionally immature parents. She also discusses her other books, which delve deeper into understanding and recovering from these experiences. Her website is Dr.LindsayGibson.com, and she offers therapist training through PESI.
