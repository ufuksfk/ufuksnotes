# Note
 China's Premier Li Keqiang delivered the 2025 government work report, outlining the country's economic goals and policy roadmap for the year.
The report set an ambitious GDP growth target of 5%, the same as 2024, despite the Chinese economy facing serious headwinds.
To achieve this target, the government plans to expand public spending by borrowing at a record level, raising the general budget deficit to 4% of GDP.
The government is also prioritizing boosting domestic consumption, which has been weak, and addressing the sluggish job market, particularly youth unemployment.
The report acknowledges the challenges facing the Chinese economy, including the property market slump, the aging population, and the potential impact of US tariffs.
