# Note
 My interview with Camilla Johannesson, Engineering Manager at Sigma Connectivity, covered her career, passions, and insights into the embedded software industry. Camilla, a Swedish native, enjoys outdoor activities and is a passionate Arsenal Women's Football fan. Her career spans over 20 years, starting as a C developer at Ericsson and progressing to her current role leading a team of software architects.

Key career highlights include contributing to Sigma Connectivity's software organization growth from 80 to 140 engineers and obtaining three patents in medical device technology. Her advice to aspiring engineers emphasizes trying new things, developing interpersonal skills, prioritizing tasks effectively, and valuing work-life balance. She stressed the importance of learning from others, adapting good practices, and discarding ineffective ones.

Camilla highlighted a concerning skills gap in embedded development, attributing it to the immediate visual feedback of web and app development. She advocates for specializing in areas like Rust programming language, Android Automotive OS, machine learning, computer vision, and cybersecurity within the automotive industry. She predicts increased connectivity and AI integration in vehicles, leading to personalized experiences and enhanced safety features like automatic emergency calls and inter-vehicle communication. Finally, she encourages a career in embedded development due to its innovative nature, global opportunities, and the chance to impact various aspects of daily life.
