# Note
 The MCC Brussels podcast discusses J.D. Vance's impactful speech at the Munich Security Conference, which asserts that the special relationship between America and Europe is over, urging Europe to take responsibility for its own security and democracy.
Vance's speech highlights the crisis of free speech in Europe and criticizes the political censorship of anti-establishment voices, emphasizing that America is watching and expects Europe to uphold democratic values.
The podcast also addresses the geopolitical shift where America is prioritizing its national interests, leading to concerns about Europe's military readiness and the implications of reduced American commitment to European security.
Additionally, the discussion reveals the EU's use of taxpayer funds to promote its political agenda through NGOs, calling for greater transparency and accountability in how these funds are allocated and spent.
