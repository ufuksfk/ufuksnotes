### ✨ Summary
The use of AI tools in education can provide personalized teaching to students who struggle with the curriculum, especially those from low socioeconomic backgrounds. Implementing AI tools can offer a cost-effective solution for personalized education, which is currently financially burdensome. Despite the potential to transform middle and high school education, the lack of demand from schools and industry disinterest hinders the development of such technologies.
