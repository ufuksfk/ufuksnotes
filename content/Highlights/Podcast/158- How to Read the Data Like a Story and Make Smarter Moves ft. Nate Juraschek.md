# Note
 Nate Juraschek is the head of product at Telaris, a B2B to B2B company. He has been working in the digital product space for 20 years, starting with music ringtones and then moving into digital transformation.
He believes that data is a tool for curiosity and that it can be used to challenge assumptions and inform decisions. He uses a combination of quantitative and qualitative data to get a complete picture of the customer journey.
He emphasizes the importance of collecting data that can be used to answer specific questions and that can be easily accessed. He recommends using tools like Heap and Logrocket for this purpose.
He believes that data can be used to celebrate success and learn from failures. He encourages teams to identify and mitigate risks, and to use data to validate assumptions.
He is excited about the potential of AI to automate data collection and analysis, and to help teams make better decisions faster. He believes that AI can help us to create better experiences for customers and to move past the digital transformation age.
