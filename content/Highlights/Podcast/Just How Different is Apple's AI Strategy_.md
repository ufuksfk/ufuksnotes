### ✨ Summary
Trust in AI often relates to concerns about privacy and the use of data for training. AI companies typically provide options to ensure data privacy and legal repercussions for breaching agreements are severe. Apple goes above and beyond by implementing measures to prevent access to user data, demonstrating a high level of commitment to privacy protection.
#### 📚 Transcript
**NLW:**
> But what most people mean by trust is the question of privacy. Will AI use what I give it as training data? And that has long been answered. All of the AI companies offer options where they agree to not use your data for training, and the legal implications for breaching these agreements would be dire. But Apple goes many steps further, putting extra work into making sure it could never learn about your data, even if it wanted to.
