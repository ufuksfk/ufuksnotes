# Note
 Flow batteries, with liquid active materials, offer advantages over lithium-ion batteries, including longer lifespan, recyclability, and reduced fire risk, making them ideal for renewable energy grids.
Vanadium flow batteries, developed since the 1980s, are now gaining traction due to the demand for long-duration energy storage, with China leading in production and installation.
Windships, utilizing wind technology for propulsion, are gaining interest for reducing greenhouse gas emissions and fuel costs, with both new builds and retrofits being explored by major shipping companies.
Off-grid solar systems in developing countries face high breakdown rates due to short warranties and lack of spare parts, leading to significant e-waste and loss of electricity access.
Efforts to improve off-grid solar repairability include advocating for modular design, supporting local repair initiatives, and integrating repair skills into education, with positive responses from companies and investors.
