# Note
 In Episode 211 of the Project MedTech podcast, Jana Montgomery, CEO of ActiVie, discusses the importance of clinical data in the medtech industry, emphasizing that it is essential for regulatory approval, reimbursement, and commercialization.
Jana shares her unique journey from aspiring radiologist to mechanical engineer and ultimately to a clinical data expert, highlighting the value of understanding both engineering and human factors in medical device development.
She advises startups to focus on the "who, what, and why" of clinical data collection, warning against the pitfalls of traditional practices and the importance of tailoring studies to meet the needs of various stakeholders.
The episode underscores the significance of choosing the right partners in clinical research, advocating for transparency and alignment of values to foster successful collaborations in the medtech space
