## Episode AI notes
1. Lego has made a pledge to become more sustainable in its manufacturing process, as reported by the Financial Times.
2. The company's head of sustainability, Tim Brooks, describes their pet plastic as a sustainable alternative to ABS for Lego bricks, emphasizing its capabilities and comparing it to making a bike out of wood instead of steel.
3. Lego's recent announcement regarding its use of petroleum-based ABS plastic has been seen as more of an excuse or kind of an apology by some.
4. The company had previously promised to eliminate petroleum-based materials by 2030 but has now decided to continue manufacturing its blocks with ABS plastic.
