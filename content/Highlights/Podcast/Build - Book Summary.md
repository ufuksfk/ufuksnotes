# Note
 In Build, [[Tony Fadell]], the co-creator of the iPod and iPhone, offers a comprehensive guide to building a successful career and company. He draws on his extensive experience in the tech industry to provide actionable advice on everything from securing a job to leading as a CEO.

Fadell emphasizes the importance of embracing the hustle of youth, surrounding yourself with passionate individuals, and relentlessly pursuing your interests. He also stresses the importance of effective team management, advocating for setting clear expectations and empowering your team to excel.

Fadell highlights the crucial role of "why" in product development and marketing, emphasizing the need to articulate the specific problems your product solves and why it matters to your audience. He also emphasizes the importance of building a diverse and dynamic team, advocating for a multigenerational workforce and a "no jerks" policy.

Finally, Fadell discusses the indispensable role of a CEO in driving excellence, emphasizing the need to embody the company's core values and vision, fostering a culture of innovation, and challenging your team to reach their highest potential.
