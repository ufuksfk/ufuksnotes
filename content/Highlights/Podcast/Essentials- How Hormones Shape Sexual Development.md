# Note
 Hormones are chemical substances released from glands that influence various organs and tissues, playing a crucial role in sexual development and differentiation from chromosomal sex to gonadal and hormonal sex.
The transition from chromosomal sex to gonadal sex involves key genes on the Y chromosome that promote the formation of testes and inhibit female reproductive structures, while hormonal sex is influenced by steroid hormones like [[testosterone]] and [[estrogen]].
Environmental factors, such as exposure to certain herbicides and substances like cannabis and alcohol, can disrupt hormone levels and sexual development, leading to significant biological and psychological effects.
Understanding the complex interplay of hormones in sexual differentiation highlights the importance of both biological and environmental influences, while also emphasizing the need for further exploration of these topics in relation to gender identity and health.
