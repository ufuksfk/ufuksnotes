- **Differentiating Pseudoscience**
  - Pseudoscience is a belief system that dresses itself up in the trappings of science to appear legitimate.
  - The inability to distinguish pseudoscience from science can cause people to lose money, resources, or even their lives.
  Transcript:
  James C. Zimring
  So understanding what science is and how it works is its own thing that is necessary for us to have a dialogue about. But pseudoscience is part of that dialogue, and it's a part of the dialogue that, in my view, is dangerous, and it victimizes people. So what pseudoscience is, in broad terms, is a system of belief that is making a claim that dresses itself up in the trappings of science with instruments that give readings and theories With abstract terms of undefined things and, you know, models and sometimes mathematics. But what it is, it is basically an attempt to take something that is baseless and make it appear as though it has legitimacy. And the inability of people to distinguish pseudoscience from science causes people to lose money, resources, and in extreme cases, it may cause people to lose their lives if they Are turning down a demonstrated medical therapy for the claims of something that are exaggerated or distorted or simply incorrect. And so, you know, nutraceuticals, other things that are largely unregulated make all manner of claim. And, you know, to your earlier point, although scientific theories of why technologies work sometimes turn out to be wrong, the technologies keep working, right? In the context of how the natural world works, science is not just another opinion.
