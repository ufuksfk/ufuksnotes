# Note
 Marty Cagan emphasizes the importance of moving from output to outcomes in product management, highlighting that outcomes are challenging but crucial for success.
Agile coaching is often misunderstood, and true transformation requires leaders who have lived the product experience to influence change effectively.
Cagan's books, such as "Inspired," "Empowered," and "Transformed," are pivotal in shaping modern product management practices.
The episode discusses the need for real product managers, designers, and engineers, emphasizing the importance of product discovery and continuous deployment.
Cagan warns against "fake agile" and stresses the significance of small, frequent releases to truly be agile.
The transformation to a product operating model involves changing how problems are identified, solved, and solutions deployed, requiring new competencies and concepts.
Cagan highlights the importance of product culture and principles, such as experimentation and tackling risks upfront, for successful product management.
The episode underscores the role of product leaders in coaching and developing teams, and the necessity of having experienced product coaches for effective transformation.
Cagan criticizes the prevalent definition of product management, which often reduces it to project management, and calls for a focus on true product management skills.
