**Plastic Composition**
- Plastic's instability arises from its composition, which breaks down into oil and gas when processed. 
- The arrangement of molecules determines the type of plastic, unlike elements like [[aluminium]].
#### 📚 Transcript
**Laura Sullivan:**
> Let's take aluminum, right? It's an element. It naturally occurs on the earth. You can melt it or heat it or smush it, and it's still going to be aluminum. This is not the same with plastic. If you break plastic down, you get oil and gas. This makes it less stable. So plastic is really just a series of molecules strung together into something called polymers. There's going to be a test on this after. And if you add molecules going in one direction, you're going to get a plastic called styrofoam. If you add the molecules in another direction, you're going to get a plastic called saran wrap. If you go another direction, you're going to get a plastic water bottle. It just depends on how you string all these different molecules together.
