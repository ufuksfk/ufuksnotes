# Note
 Sweden is reacting to a significant shift in U.S. foreign policy, particularly following Vice President JD Vance's controversial speech at the Munich Security Conference, which criticized European allies, including Sweden.
Swedish politicians, including Foreign Minister Maria Malmer Steinergard, have diplomatically rebuffed Vance's claims, emphasizing Sweden's commitment to free speech and the independence of its judicial system.
Amidst the political turmoil, Sweden's support for Ukraine remains strong, with leaders expressing concern over U.S.-Russia negotiations that exclude Ukraine and Europe from the discussion.
The closure of the Internationale Engelska school in Stockholm has sparked emotional responses from students and teachers, with calls for the city to take over the school's management to ensure students can complete their education.
