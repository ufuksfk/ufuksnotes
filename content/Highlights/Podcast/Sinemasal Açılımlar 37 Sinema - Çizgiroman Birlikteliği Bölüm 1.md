# Note
 This week's episode of Sinemasal Açılımlar covers the relationship between cinema and comic books. It's a large topic, so it will be covered in two parts. The first part focuses on the history of comic books, starting with their origins in 1896 as newspaper comic strips.

The early history of comic books, particularly in America, is intertwined with their image and perceived value. Initially seen as lowbrow entertainment, their status changed with the success of Superman in 1938 and the work of Will Eisner in the 1940s. The rise and fall of comic book popularity is linked to events like World War II and subsequent social changes.

The history of American comics mirrors that of cinema, both emerging around the same time and facing similar periods of censorship and fluctuating popularity. Both initially viewed as lowbrow entertainment, they both achieved artistic recognition in the 1940s. The first comic book adaptation for film, Flash Gordon, appeared in 1936, predating even Superman's comic debut. This first part concludes with a promise to continue the discussion of cinema and comic books in the next episode.
