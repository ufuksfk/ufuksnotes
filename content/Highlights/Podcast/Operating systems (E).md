This practice highlights a difference in operational norms in various regions, as many areas in Europe do not adopt this rule, illustrating how urban traffic regulations can vary significantly across cultures.

### ✨ Summary
Transportation significantly influences human movement, paralleling the commitment levels associated with operating systems in computing. When users choose an operating system, they are essentially bound to it, limiting their options compared to software applications where alternatives may be readily available. This lock-in effect means that changes made by the operating system provider can disrupt functionality for users, leading to significant frustration when their experience is altered without warning. Therefore, once committed to an operating system, individuals confront consequences that stem from that choice, especially when updates or changes are implemented unilaterally by the provider.
