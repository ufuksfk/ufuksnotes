# Note
I picked [[How to Win Friends and Influence People]] by [[Dale Carnegie]] because I wanted to explore a book with polarizing opinions. It's an older book, published in [[1937]], but the principles are timeless and relevant today. The book is broken down into four sections: Fundamental Techniques in Handling People, Six Ways to Make People Like You, How to Win People to Your Way of Thinking, and Be a Leader.

I found the book to be very helpful, especially in the context of building relationships and influencing people. I was particularly struck by the distinction between flattery and appreciation, and the importance of being genuinely interested in other people. I also appreciated the emphasis on avoiding arguments and finding ways to criticize people without giving offense.

I think this book is a great introduction to the concepts of building relationships and influencing people. It's not a deep dive into any one topic, but it provides a solid foundation for further exploration. I would recommend this book to anyone who wants to improve their communication skills and build stronger relationships.
