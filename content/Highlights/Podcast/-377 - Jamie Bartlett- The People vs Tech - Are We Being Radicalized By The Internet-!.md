# Note
 The internet is radicalizing people by pushing them towards extremes. This is due to the use of engagement-based algorithms that prioritize content that will generate clicks and ad revenue. These algorithms are designed to keep users hooked and scrolling, which can lead to people being bombarded with content that reinforces their existing beliefs and biases. This can create echo chambers where people are only exposed to information that confirms their worldview, leading to increased polarization and anger.

The problem is exacerbated by the fact that social media platforms are increasingly becoming video-centric, which makes content more emotional and impactful. This allows for the spread of misinformation and disinformation through the use of emotionally charged videos and anecdotes. The right has been more successful than the left at using this tactic to caricature their opponents and create a sense of outrage and fear.

The solution is not to censor or ban these platforms, but to teach media literacy and encourage people to consume a wider range of information. This includes understanding how algorithms work, recognizing cognitive biases, and being critical of the information we consume. We need to move away from the idea that social media is a marketplace of ideas and recognize that it can actually drive us apart.
