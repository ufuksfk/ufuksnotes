# Note
 Digital reading processes differ from reading on paper, with digital reading being faster and more superficial, leading to potential comprehension issues.
Audiobooks have become increasingly popular, especially for multitasking, but may not offer the same cognitive benefits as traditional reading.
Schools are transitioning to digital texts for economic reasons, but may not be adequately addressing the differences in reading comprehension between digital and paper formats.
