## Highlights
- **Arc'teryx In-Store Repair Space**
  - Arc'teryx provides a dedicated ReBird repair space in its flagship store's basement.
  - The space offers free small repairs and washing/re-application of DWR to extend garment life.
  Transcript:
  Pippa
  And repairs are sent to an off-site repair facility, a large section of the store's basement floor has been given over to the ReBird repair space where the team can carry out small repairs For free.   Adam
  So this is where the magic happens. This is our main attraction in this service centre. Here we can wash all of our technical garments from our Gore-Tex specific products to our down products to our soft shells. All of our garments do require washing. In fact, washing the garments will extend the life of a product. Is it like the first step to circularity?
  Pippa
  Absolutely. This is the first step to circularity.

  So we wash the garments and then we can also reapply them, reapply the DWR, excuse me. And what's DWR? DWR is the durable water repellency that we apply to all of our garments. That specific treatment helps also repel off garments rather than absorb into the fabric.
