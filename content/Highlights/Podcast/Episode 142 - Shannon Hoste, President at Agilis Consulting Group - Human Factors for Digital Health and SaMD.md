# Note
 Shannon Hoste, President of Agilis Consulting Group, discusses her extensive background in medtech, focusing on human factors testing for software as a medical device and digital health, emphasizing the importance of understanding user interactions.
The conversation highlights the evolution of human factors regulations, particularly the FDA's 2016 guidance and IEC 62366 standards, which have made human factors assessments a mandatory part of the medical device approval process.
Hoste explains the differences between human factors in physical devices and digital health, noting that while physical devices focus on ergonomic interactions, digital health emphasizes information hazards and user decision-making.
She shares insights from her experience at Enable Injections, a company innovating in drug delivery, stressing the complexities of combining pharmaceutical and device regulations and the importance of a user-centric approach in product development.
