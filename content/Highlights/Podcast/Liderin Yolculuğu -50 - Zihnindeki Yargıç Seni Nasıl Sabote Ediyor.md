# Note
 This episode of Lider'in Yolculuğu podcast focuses on the "Judge" saboteur, a key concept in positive intelligence, which describes the inner critic that constantly judges ourselves, others, and situations.
The Judge saboteur is a universal saboteur, present in everyone, and is often programmed to speak negatively. It thrives on creating an idealized version of reality and comparing ourselves to it, leading to feelings of inadequacy and suffering.
The Judge saboteur's journey begins with self-criticism, often stemming from childhood experiences where we felt unloved or unworthy. This leads to a cycle of seeking validation through pleasing others, striving for perfection, or playing the victim.
As we grow, the Judge saboteur's focus shifts to judging others, often as a defense mechanism to protect our own perceived flaws. This can lead to a constant state of blame and criticism.
The Judge saboteur's ultimate goal is to protect us, but its methods can be detrimental to our well-being. It can negatively impact our health, happiness, and success.
