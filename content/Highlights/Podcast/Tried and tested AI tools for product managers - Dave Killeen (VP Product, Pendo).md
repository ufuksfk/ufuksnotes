# Note
 My three-month exploration of AI tools during a sabbatical revealed incredible time-saving potential. I now save about a day a week, primarily through voice-to-text input for writing and presentations. This increased efficiency allows me to focus on higher-level tasks.

Tools like Claude and Super Whisperer are game-changers for boosting productivity. Using voice input significantly increases my writing speed, translating to hours of saved time weekly. I treat these AI tools conversationally, even thanking them, which has become intuitive.

Protecting my time is crucial, and Sunsama helps me manage my calendar and tasks effectively. I allocate specific time blocks for focused work and use gaming music to maintain energy levels. This approach, combined with AI-powered task management, maximizes my daily output.

Amplifying my impact involves crafting detailed prompts for LLMs. [[Anthropic]]'s prompt generation tool significantly improves the quality of AI responses. Chorus allows me to use multiple LLMs simultaneously, synthesizing their outputs for superior results. Claude projects help maintain context across multiple sessions.

Agentic AI, exemplified by Gumloop, allows me to connect various data sources (competitor analysis, customer feedback) to AI for insightful analysis. This enables proactive identification of market trends and competitor activities, informing strategic decisions. The emergence of MCP (Model Context Protocol) will further revolutionize how AI agents interact and share data, impacting product strategy significantly.

I believe that staying current with AI tools is essential for career advancement. Ignoring this technological shift will likely lead to a contraction in the product management job market. The ability to effectively utilize AI, beyond basic tools like ChatGPT, is becoming a critical skill. I encourage everyone to explore these tools and integrate them into their workflows.
