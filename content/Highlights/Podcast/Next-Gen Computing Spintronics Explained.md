- **Spin vs Charge**
  - Traditional electronics relies on electron charge to process data, which consumes energy and generates heat. 
  - Spintronics uses electron spin, which requires less energy because spin stays in place, potentially revolutionizing computing.

- **Spintronics and Quantum Tunneling**
  - Spintronic devices leverage quantum tunneling, which becomes problematic for classical transistors at extremely small scales. 
  - By using quantum tunneling instead of fighting it, spintronics becomes ideal for sub-nano scale operations.
