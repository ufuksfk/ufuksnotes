## Episode AI notes
1. The executive order on AI, announced by President Biden, imposes regulations on various aspects of new AI models. The order recognizes the significant impact of AI in society and requires companies to share internal testing data and notify the government when training new frontier foundation models.
2. The executive order exposes the inconsistency of tech companies' positions on AI regulation. CEOs from major tech companies expressed support for regulating AI during the Schumer AI Insight Forum, leading to headlines endorsing AI regulation.
3. To bring about change and create new paths, we must believe that we are at the starting point of new hope and opportunity. Paths are not already set, but are formed as we take action and create our own paths.
