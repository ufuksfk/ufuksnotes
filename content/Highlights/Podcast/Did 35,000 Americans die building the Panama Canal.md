#### ✨ Summary
Fernando Lesseps, the acclaimed builder of the Suez Canal, launched a project in 1880 to construct a sea-level canal in Panama, capturing the support of the French public seeking a venture to restore national pride post the Franco-Prussian War. Despite not having visited Panama, Lesseps committed to a lockless design similar to the Suez Canal, highlighting his ambition to attain a smoother canal construction and operation. This decision marked the inception of a monumental engineering failure.
#### 📚 Transcript
**Speaker 1:**
> Fernando Lesseps, the builder of the Suez Canal, Le Grons Francais, described as the presiding genius of the 19th century. And when was this? This is in 1880. He sets up a company, raises money from the patriotic French public who see this as something that will be to the glory of France and re-established its pride. They of course recently lost the Franco-Prussian War and they rush out to Panama and decide to build a sea level canal, like the Suez Canal. The Lesseps decided this before he'd even been to Panama because this was easier to sell. No one wants locks. You want to just sail straight through as you can at Suez. And this was really the beginning of one of the great engineering disasters in history.

### ✨ Summary
In the early days, various theories existed about the cause of yellow fever, from winds to apple consumption. Treatment involved mustard, seed, brandy, and cigars. Yellow fever granted immunity to survivors. However, the Panama Canal project attracted a diverse unimmune population, leading to devastating yellow fever outbreaks caused by mosquitoes.
#### 📚 Transcript
**Speaker 1:**
> Others thought it was a particular wind off the sea. One doctor insisted it was from eating apples. And it was treated in those days with mustard, seed, brandy and cigars. And this disease requires unimmune victims. So if you get yellow fever and you survive, you are then subsequently immune. But what the canal project did, of course, was bring in lots and lots of people from around the world and created this population of unimmune people. And the yellow fever mosquitoes had a devastating effect.
