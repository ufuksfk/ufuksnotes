# Note
 This episode discusses China's growing influence in Africa, particularly through its space investments.
China has signed agreements with 23 African countries to finance satellite projects and build ground stations, increasing its soft power in the region.
The episode highlights the competition between China and the US in space exploration, with China's "International Lunar Research Station" project posing a challenge to the US's "Artemis" program.
The episode explores the historical context of China's involvement in Africa, dating back to the 1960s, and its current economic and political influence.
The episode discusses the potential for China's growing influence in Africa to lead to a "debt trap diplomacy" scenario, where African countries become reliant on Chinese loans and investments.
