#### ✨ Summary
IoT devices should have regular firmware updates with security patches, ideally done over the air. Understanding the device's operating system, capabilities, and potential attack risks is important. Following a zero trust methodology is advised, assuming that devices are malicious and working backward from there, rather than assuming the opposite.
