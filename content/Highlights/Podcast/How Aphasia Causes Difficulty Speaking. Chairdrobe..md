### ✨ Summary
Global aphasia represents the most severe form of aphasia, leading to muteness in patients. In comparison, less severe types of aphasia retain some language abilities and are categorized into two distinct types: fluent aphasia, characterized by the production of speech with normal grammar and vocabulary, and non-fluent aphasia, which involves limited speech output and challenges in forming grammatically correct sentences.
