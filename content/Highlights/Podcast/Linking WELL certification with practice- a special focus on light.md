### **WELL v2 Certification**

So, same keywords again :) Circadian rhythms, daylight over electrical light.

WELL v2 introduced changes to make the certification more feasible and meaningful, especially around preconditions for lighting. Circadian lighting requirements became more flexible, allowing projects to provide light exposure through electric light or daylight. WELL v2 focuses on providing a visually comfortable environment and maintaining circadian rhythms through light exposure.

It is kind of popular certification with different adoption rates in different countries. IWBI plans to update certification features to include more options for daylight exposure and visual balance, they are also organizing more educations to industry to increase knowledge.

From the 2021 (when they record that episode) to today, they keep updating WELL v2, and you can check it from their website.[2](#footnote-2)

And it is not only light, but also air, water, thermal comfort, movement, materials and so on. It is a well-known comprehensive certification.
