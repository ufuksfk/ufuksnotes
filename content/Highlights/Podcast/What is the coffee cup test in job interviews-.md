# Note
 The coffee cup test, a hiring practice gaining renewed attention, involves observing whether a candidate disposes of their coffee cup after an interview. Proponents, like Trent Innes, argue it assesses soft skills and a sense of ownership, crucial for workplace culture. However, the test's effectiveness is debated, with some criticizing it as manipulative and a sign of poor management.


## Highlights
* [[2025-04#19]]  Some of these skills may be innate, but they can also be developed or acquired through other activities like arts or sports. Traits like empathy, active listening and self reflection help in managing relationships with colleagues.

* [[2025-04#19]]  Are we saying the coffee cup test is genuinely effective then? Trent Innes himself maintains that the recruitment method is highly effective, but online opinion is split.
