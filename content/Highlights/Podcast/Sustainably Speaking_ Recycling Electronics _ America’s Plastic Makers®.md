**Joining Evterra**
- Cody Oliver joined Evterra Recycling after talking to its CEO and realizing he wanted to contribute to their goal of becoming one of the largest electronics recyclers in the US. 
- This led to new initiatives for plastics and the future of plastics recycling.
#### 📚 Transcript
**Mia Quinn:**
> That sort of led you to what you're doing at Eftera.
**Cody Oliver:**
> Yeah. When I was looking at downstream partners, I was kind of introduced to this new company, Evtera Recycling, and met with the CEO, Diane Dieselhorse, and really was talking to him about His business. And they were trying to grow into one of the largest electronics recyclers in the US. And the light bulb went off and I asked, hey, I would like to join your team and how can I contribute? So it really kind of led into a lot of these new initiatives for plastics and the future of plastics recycling.
**Mia Quinn:**
> You remove plastic when you're recycling electronics. And then once you remove it, what do you do with it?
**Cody Oliver:**
> There's a couple of different options. One is for smaller folks that don't have industrial separation equipment, right? They'll have a team with hammers and drills and cutting tools and whatever it takes to remove those casings. But the secondary way, which a lot of folks do, and it's what we do, is really a large shredding operation.
