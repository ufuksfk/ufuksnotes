#### ✨ Summary
It is crucial to reject the mindset of competing due to perceived external pressures. This philosophical trap fosters a cycle of paranoia where nations feel compelled to act in certain ways just because others might. By stepping back from this mentality, it is possible to break free from the cycle of reactive behavior and approach decisions with a more thoughtful and independent perspective.
