# Note
 FDA began regulating medical devices in 1976, with software starting to appear in devices over the next decade.
FDA developed guidance documents in the 1990s for software in medical devices, focusing on risk-based frameworks and documentation requirements.
FDA's approach evolved to include off-the-shelf software in medical devices, down-classifying certain devices over time, and exercising enforcement discretion for general wellness products.
The agency's regulation extended to wearables with sensors, allowing for non-medical device functions, leading to the approval of software-only products like ECG apps for Apple Watch and sleep apnea features for Samsung watches.
