# Note
 LillyDirect is a patient care resource offering support for managing chronic conditions like migraines, diabetes, and obesity. They provide options to connect patients with telehealth providers, offer obesity and wellness education, and deliver prescribed medications directly to your door.
The future of healthcare technology involves bringing technology into everything at Eli Lilly and Company. Diogo Rau, the Chief Information and Digital Officer, aims to change the industry by focusing on consumer orientation and improving the overall patient experience.
Success for Diogo Rau at Lilly involves making a mark on both the consumer side and the discovery side of medicine. By improving swiftness in delivering medications and utilizing AI for discovering new medicines, millions of lives can be positively impacted.
