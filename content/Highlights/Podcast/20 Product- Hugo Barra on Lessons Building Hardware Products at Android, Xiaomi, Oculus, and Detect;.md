* [[2025-06]]  Chinese culture is to really, really go out of your way to make sure people always feel respected. In China you have this age old concept of face, which is essentially a person's social dignity to be a little dramatic.

* [[2025-06]]  If you make someone feel like they've given, for example, wrong answer or offered a silly idea in a meeting, you could cause them to lose face.

* [[2025-06]]  So in practice, what this means is that if you need to have a difficult conversation or give someone strong feedback, do that in private. * [[2025-06]]  call These two different approaches, Feature King and Budget King. Feature King are products that have all the features and are for that reason more expensive.
