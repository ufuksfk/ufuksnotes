## Notes

### **Background and Project Aim:**

- Sebastian Knoche, a former academic with a doctoral degree in theoretical soft metaphysics, works in the light and design industry focusing on sustainability.
    
- He is involved in the Reprolite project, aimed at making the European lighting industry more sustainable and competitive.
    

### **Environmental Burdens and Lifecycle Consideration:**

- The project emphasized the lifecycle analysis of luminaires, considering material mining, production, usage, and disposal.
    
- Two main resources in the lifecycle analysis: energy consumption and material usage.
    
    - **Energy usage**: 1% in the production phase and 99% in the use phase.
        
    - **Material usage**: ADP (Abiotic Depletion Potential) elements, measuring the preciousness of materials. Production phase contributes to 75% of the ADP elements, and the use phase contributes to 25%.
        

### **Key Takeaways on Sustainability:**

- **Energy vs. Material Efficiency**: Improving one can sometimes worsen the other. Maximizing both simultaneously is challenging.
    
- **LED Module Replacement**: Frequent replacements for energy efficiency lead to material waste. Longer usage, however, reduces energy efficiency due to luminous efficacy degradation.
    
- **Lifecycle Impact of Light Management Systems**: Significant energy savings with daylight and presence sensors, with savings of around 25% for daylight control and 5% for presence detection.
    

### **Design and Sustainability:**

- **Exchangeability of LED Modules**: Not necessarily beneficial for CO2 reduction or material usage reduction.
    
- **Failure Rates**: Very low in high-quality lighting products. Only 1% of fixtures are estimated to fail before reaching their nominal lifetime.
    

### **Eco Design Directives and Lifetimes:**

- Echo design directives may enforce replaceability, which might not always make sense.
    
- LED luminaires can have technical lifetimes far exceeding the refurbishment cycles of the spaces they illuminate, posing challenges for sustainability.
    

### **Sustainability in Practice:**

- **Second Life for Luminaires**: There's interest in using secondhand or upcycled luminaires, but challenges exist in establishing a market and addressing warranty issues.
    
- **Material Efficiency and Product Design**: Emphasis on reducing raw material use in luminaire design and considering the entire lifecycle impact, including possibilities for easier recycling or reuse.
    

### **Consumer Behavior and Sustainability:**

- The quote from Huari O'Brien, "Everything that is loved is sustainable," highlights the importance of valuing quality and longevity in products to improve sustainability across industries, not just in lighting.
    

### **Personal Reflection:**

- Sebastian Knoche shared a personal light memory related to observing soap bubbles as a child, linking his fascination with natural phenomena and physics.
