Designing for RF, medical imaging, or precision sensors? Don't let power supply noise ruin your signal.  
  
For the most demanding applications, clean power is non-negotiable. While a switcher and LDO post-regulator is a common solution, it's bulky, complex, and still carries the risk of radiated EMI.  
  
Our BK30 series was designed for this challenge. By co-packaging our enhanced linear regulator (ELR) technology with a state-of-the-art, ultra-low-noise LDO from [Analog Devices](https://www.linkedin.com/company/analog-devices/), we deliver:  
-- Efficiency comparable to a switching + post-regulator solution (60-85%, depending on step).  
-- Peak PSRR values exceeding 120dB.  
-- Integrated output noise below 1µV(RMS).  
-- All in a single, switching-free package.
