## Highlights
- In May 2022, United States President [Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden) and Japanese Prime Minister [Fumio Kishida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fumio_Kishida) met in Tokyo and discussed increased collaboration on important technologies, including semiconductors, [nuclear power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_power), [space exploration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_exploration), [electric batteries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_batteries), [critical minerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_elements_facing_shortage), and [supply chains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supply_chains).[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-10) The [Second Kishida Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Kishida_Cabinet)'s basic policy (*[Honebuto no hōshin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honebuto_no_h%C5%8Dshin)*) announced on 7 June 2022 included consideration of support for private companies developing next-generation advanced technologies and the establishment of a design and manufacturing infrastructure for next-generation semiconductors in the latter half of the 2020s.
- The [Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economy,_Trade_and_Industry) (METI) has decided to establish the [Leading-edge Semiconductor Technology Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading-edge_Semiconductor_Technology_Center) (LSTC) as an organization to conduct next-generation semiconductor research in anticipation of joint research between the two countries based on the basic principles of semiconductor cooperation between Japan and the United States agreed on in May.
- On 6 December 2022, the Belgium-based [Interuniversity Microelectronics Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interuniversity_Microelectronics_Centre) (IMEC) announced it had signed a Memorandum of Cooperation with Rapidus, where both companies will set up a long-term and sustainable collaboration on advanced semiconductor technologies, while Rapidus would plan to mass-produce chips in 2-nanometer technology in Japan in the latter half of 2020s.[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-20)[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-21)[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-22)[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-23)
- In July 2023, Rapidus and [Tokyo Electron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Electron) were discussed as companies that would "play a major role" in a new [memorandum of understanding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorandum_of_understanding) between Japan and [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India) that is expected to involve $35.9 billion of investment by Japan in India by 2027[[37]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-37) and was officially signed in [New Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi) by ministers Yasutoshi Nishimura and [Ashwini Vaishnaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashwini_Vaishnaw) for their countries respectively.
- Dutch equipment manufacturer [ASML Holding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASML_Holding) announced they would also open an office in Chitose with 40-50 technical staff to assist Rapidus.[[44]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-44) While full operation is not expected until 2027, a [pilot line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilot_line) is expected to start in 2025.
- On 17 November 2023, Rapidus signed an agreement with the AI computing company [Tenstorrent Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Tenstorrent_Inc.&action=edit&redlink=1) from [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto), Canada.[[46]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-46)
- According to a February 27, 2024 [Bloomberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_L.P.) article, Rapidus announced that it has been contracted to produce next-generation AI chips from Canadian startup Tenstorrent.[[48]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-48) Tenstorrent is the first customer announced by Rapidus.[[49]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-49)[[50]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-50) The architecture that Tenstorrent outsourced to Rapidus for production and development is based on [RISC-V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RISC-V), and its benefits are close to what the university stakeholders, who are the main members of Japan's Leading-edged Semiconductor Technology Center (LSTC), desire (because of its open [Instruction set architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instruction_set_architecture)). Furthermore, the biggest challenge in the relevant architecture is said to be the optimization of the calculation pipeline, and maximizing microfabrication technology is also an important issue in order to realize a multi-stage pipeline.
- On April 11, the company announced the establishment of a new company, Rapidus Design Solutions, in Silicon Valley, United States. Henri Richard was appointed president. In addition to selling to major American IT companies, Rapidus Design Solutions (RDS) aim to secure software and design engineers to create semiconductors that meet customer requests.[[53]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-53) The expansion of RDS in the U.S. is also to build upon Rapidus's already existing presence there, with it having more than 100 engineers and scientists working at the Albany NanoTech Complex facility in New York.[[54]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-54)
- The Japanese government allocated 802.5 billion yen (US$5.4 billion) in subsidies for Rapidus in fiscal year 2025.[[56]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-56)[[57]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidus#cite_note-57)
