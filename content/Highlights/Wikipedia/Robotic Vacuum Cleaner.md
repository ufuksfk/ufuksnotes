## Highlights
- In [[1956]], the American science fiction author [Robert A. Heinlein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_A._Heinlein) described the concept of a robotic vacuum cleaner with a recharging dock in his novel *[The Door into Summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Door_into_Summer)*: "Basically it was just a better vacuum cleaner
- In [[1969]] on 2 April an episode of *[The Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Avengers_(TV_programme))* was broadcast in which the character Inge Tilson played by Dora Reisser says "...I saw a demonstration once. A robot vacuum cleaner. It swept around the house, went back into its cupboard, automatically plugged in and recharged itself...".
- In [[2015]], [[Dyson]] and iRobot both introduced camera-based mapping.
- These are [open-source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_source_hardware) designs that can be built using off-the-shelf components and [3D printed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3D_printing) parts.

  • Open Autonomous Domestic Robots by Keith Elliott (3D printing, scrap electronics, 2016)[[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotic_vacuum_cleaner/#cite_note-32)
  • DIY Vacuum Robot by CesNieto (3D printing, Arduino Uno, 2017)[[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotic_vacuum_cleaner/#cite_note-33)[[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotic_vacuum_cleaner/#cite_note-34)
