## Highlights
- The **Matthew effect of accumulated advantage**, sometimes called the **Matthew principle**, is the tendency of individuals to accrue social or economic success in proportion to their initial level of popularity, friends, and wealth.
- The Matthew effect may largely be explained by [preferential attachment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preferential_attachment), whereby wealth or credit is distributed among individuals according to how much they already have.
