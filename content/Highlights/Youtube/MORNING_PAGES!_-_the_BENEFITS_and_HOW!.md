- 📚
    
    Morning pages are a technique from Julia Cameron's book The Artist's Way, and they can be a valuable tool for self-reflection and making the most of one's thoughts and ideas.
    
- 📝
    
    Morning pages are considered the "bedrock" of a writer's work, emphasizing their importance in the creative process.
    
- 🖊
    
    ️ "One of the most important aspects of writing morning pages is that you don't stop. You just keep the pen or pencil moving across the page."
    
- 🤔
    
    Morning pages provide an opportunity to break free from the fear of judgment and self-censorship, allowing us to express ourselves authentically.
