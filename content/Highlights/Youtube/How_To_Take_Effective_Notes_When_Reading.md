- Having the right reading accessories, such as a notebook, pen, and highlighter, can enhance the effectiveness of note-taking while reading.
- 🖋
    
    ️ Having a question in mind before starting to read helps ensure that the information obtained from the book is relevant to that specific question.
    
- 💡
    
    While reading, it's important to highlight and write down any ideas that resonate with you from the book.
    
- 💡
    
    Engage actively in the reading process by jotting down notes in the margins or highlighting ideas and quotes that resonate with you.
