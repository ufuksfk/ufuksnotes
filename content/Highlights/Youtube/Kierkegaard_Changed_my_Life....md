We're always somewhat in despair. Some people try to numb their despair with central pleasure in the moment, which will never suffice. This is also called the aesthetic sphere in Kierkegaard's philosophy. Others try to get rid of their despair by acting ethically, but that won't do the trick either, because acting ethically is not related to the infinite. This sickness is a sickness of the infinite. And how can you cure a sickness of the infinite? Only with a medicine of the infinite. God.
