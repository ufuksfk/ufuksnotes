I am taking that course at [[2025-02#03]] about [[Product Management]] and [[Technical Product Management]]

If you are problem solver, you found your passion!
there is no straightforward way to become a TPM.

Lecturer: [[Marily Nika]], AI Product Lead at [[Google]] and Fellow at [[Harvard Business School]]

What the role means?
You need a technical influence to your engineer colleagues. Just you need to have passion to solve technical problems.
A PM helps their team build and ship the **right product.**
Vision and empathy.
* Who are your users?
* What are their goals, aspirations, pain points and use cases?
Come up with user stories!
Is your product and market fit?

Minimum working product with maximum possible learning. You need to create your first iteration to learn! Learn and analyze it, and iterate something better.

### Skillset?
* Hard skills
	* Abilities that can be demonstrated in a measured way
	* Translating user needs into product requirements
	* Analytical skills ( it is hard skill here? :D )
* Soft skills
	* Empathy
	* Long term vision
	* Strategy, long term vision
	* Technical influence (it is soft skill here :) )
	* Storyteller

Most important skill is being a janitor. not being storyteller.

She asked are you creative and are you coming up great ideas every day! She said she is :)
Whenever you have a new idea, be sure it has a purpose.

Good idea will find product market fit, if your defined user segment needs it. They should pay for it as well.

Avoid shiny object trap!
Make sure you are solving a problem
Look into the competition
Come up with a plan
Build a roadmap

Make sure to sit close to them!
* Count on your team's skills
* Don't micromanage
* Trust them!

### Find user problems, pain points, use cases
Create Persona
* What are their goals and motivations
* Describe them! Who they are?
* What are use cases for them with your product
* Pain points of each of user persona
### Metrics
* quantitative
	* engagement
	* retention
	* revenue
* qualitative
	* User satisfaction
		* NPS or CSAT score
