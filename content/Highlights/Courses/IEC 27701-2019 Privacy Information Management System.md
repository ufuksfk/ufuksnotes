Requirements for a Privacy Information Management System
[[IEC 27701]] Extension for privacy of [[IEC 27001]]
Aligned with [[(GDPR) General Data Protection Regulation]]
Other related standards: [[IEC 29100]] [[IEC 29151]] [[IEC 29134]]

[[(PII) Personal Identifiable Information]]
Controls for PII controllers
Controls for PII processors

Actors according to [[IEC 29100]]:
* Joint PII controller
	* PII principals
		* person who provide personal data for processing
	* PII controller
		* determines the purpose and means of PII processing
	* PII processor
		* Carries out processing of PII on behalf of the controller and according to its instructions

Organization can be both PII controller and PII processor, but not for the same processing activity
