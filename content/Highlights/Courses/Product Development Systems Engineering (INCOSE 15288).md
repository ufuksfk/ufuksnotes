### System Defined
Based on [[IEEE 15288]]:
System: "A combination of interacting elements organized to achieve one or more stated purposes"

Integrated: "A collection or aggregate of system elements, working elements"
Elements: can be product, facility, people, processes, services, techniques
### System Hierarchy
Organizational representation of a system structure using partitioning relationship
System - Subsystem - Component - Sub component - Part
these diagrams have some languages like uml, sysml

System Environment = External entities that the system interacts with in a specified context

### System Boundaries
It is within the system boundaries if:
* The organization has control over the entity's behaviors, development, requirements & funding
* Your organization has operational control of the entity after development. Missions are directed by the owner of the entity.
* Your organization owns the design details, functional & physical architectures.
