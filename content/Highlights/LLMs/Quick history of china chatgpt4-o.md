[[History of China]]
**[[1949]] – Founding of the People's Republic of China (PRC)**
- [[Mao Zedong]] proclaims PRC after victory in Chinese Civil War.
- Communists defeat Nationalists (who flee to Taiwan).

**1950–1953 – Korean War**
- China intervenes to support [[North Korea]] against South Korea and UN forces.
- Boosts Mao’s authority but strains economy.

**1953–1957 – First Five-Year Plan**
- Soviet-style heavy industrialization.
- Some success, but agriculture struggles.

**[[1958]]–[[1962]] – Great Leap Forward**
- Mao’s campaign to rapidly industrialize (backyard furnaces, communes).
- Massive famine – estimated 15–45 million deaths.
- Major failure; Mao loses some political power.

**1966–1976 – Cultural Revolution**
- Mao regains power, launches revolution to purge "capitalist" elements.
- Red Guards (radical youth) attack intellectuals, officials, traditions.
- Chaos: schools closed, economy disrupted, millions persecuted.

**[[1971]] – [[China]] joins the UN**
- Replaces [[Taiwan]] as the official Chinese representative at the UN.

**[[1972]] – [[Richard Nixon]] visits China**
- Start of U.S.–China rapprochement during the Cold War.

**[[1976]] – Death of Mao Zedong**
- End of Cultural Revolution.
- Power struggle: "Gang of Four" (radical Maoists) arrested soon after.

**[[1978]] – [[Deng Xiaoping]] begins "Reform and Opening Up"**
- Economic liberalization: market reforms, foreign investment, rural de-collectivization.
- Rapid economic growth begins.
- Political control remains tight (Communist Party still rules).

**[[1989]] – Tiananmen Square Massacre**
- Pro-democracy protests crushed by military.
- Global condemnation but [[(CCP) China Communist Party]] retains control.

**1992 – Deng's "Southern Tour"**
- Reaffirms commitment to economic reforms despite political hard-lining.

**[[2001]] – [[China]] joins the [[World Trade Organization (WTO)]]**
- Huge boost to economy; China becomes the "world’s factory."

**[[2000s]] – Economic boom**
- Double-digit GDP growth.
- Rise of tech giants (Alibaba, Tencent), huge urbanization.

**2012 – [[Xi Jinping]] becomes leader**
- Starts **anti-corruption campaign** (also consolidates his own power).
- Pushes for “Chinese Dream” of national revival.

**2018 – Xi removes presidential term limits**
- Effectively allowing him to rule indefinitely.

**2020 – COVID-19 pandemic starts in Wuhan**
- Strict lockdowns, "Zero-COVID" policy until late 2022.

**2022 – Economic challenges and protests**
- "White Paper Protests" against harsh lockdowns.
- Government shifts away from Zero-COVID.

**Today – China under Xi Jinping**
- Tense U.S.–China relations (trade war, tech rivalry, Taiwan tensions).
- Focus on high-tech industries (AI, chips, green energy).
- Growing nationalism and tighter political control.
