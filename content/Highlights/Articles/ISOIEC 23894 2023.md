## Highlights
- ISO/IEC 23894:2023. The standard provides guidance on managing risk related to AI, but it is not a certification standard
- Not mandatory: ISO/IEC 23894:2023 is not a mandatory standard, meaning that organizations are not required to follow it. As such, it may not be adopted by all organizations developing and using AI, which could limit its impact.
- ISO/IEC 23894:2023 provides strategic guidance for managing risks connected to the development and use of AI across all sectors
