## Highlights
- In the [[1930s]], while studying [switching circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switching_circuit), [NEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NEC) engineer Akira Nakashima independently discovered [Boolean algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_algebra), which he was unaware of until 1938. In a series of papers published from 1934 to 1936, he formulated a [two-valued Boolean algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-element_Boolean_algebra) as a way to analyze and design circuits by [algebraic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra) means in terms of [logic gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic_gate).[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_science_and_technology_in_Japan#cite_note-historical-16)[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_science_and_technology_in_Japan#cite_note-Stanković-Astola_2008-17)
