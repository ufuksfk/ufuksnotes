## Highlights
- [[Fakir Hausgerate]] has been established by H. Wilhelm Kicherer in [[1933]], in Stuttgart [[Germany]]. “Fakir” brand was born from the combination of Familie which means family in German and the surname of its founder Kicherer.
- The company, which continued to grow within Germany until [[1978]], met Turkish consumers by entering the Turkish market with the growth decision taken in [[1985]], through [[Saruhan Holding]]’s assurance.
- With its superior German technology, Fakir Hausgeräte meets consumers in 80 countries around the world today. By continuously increasing customer satisfaction with its reliable, high quality and innovative products in all its markets, it continues to maintain its leadership in the sector. In addition to concept Fakir Shops reaching 20 in number, Fakir Hausgeräte spread over Turkey with over 7000 sales points and with its online sales shop, creating difference thanks to its strong technical service network and plus services.
