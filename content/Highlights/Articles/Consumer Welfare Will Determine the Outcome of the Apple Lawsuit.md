## Highlights
- The government’s monopolization [complaint](https://www.justice.gov/d9/2024-03/420763.pdf) against [[Apple]] challenges iPhone designs that exclude some applications from competitors. These include apps that compete with iMessaging, the Apple Watch, Apple’s wallet, cloud storage, and others. [[Wiki/Others/Monopoly]]
- Apple earns high margins on [[iPhone]] sales, more than double versus rivals in the industry.
- The complaint then alleges that Apple’s market share exceeds 70% in the “performance” market and 65% of the broader smartphone market in the [[USA]]. [Worldwide](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/worldwide), the numbers look much different. Apple’s share is under 30% and [[Android]]’s exceeds 70%. In the U.S., a wealthier country, a higher percentage of people choose the iPhone.
- An approach encumbered with fewer restrictive legal rules would treat Apple’s exclusion of competing apps as technological ties, or “tech ties.” These are [product designs that tie](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/603/263/105215/) a primary product (in this case, the iPhone) and a secondary product (e.g., smartwatches) together, [excluding or disfavoring rivals](https://www.yalelawjournal.org/article/the-antitrust-duty-to-deal-in-the-age-of-big-tech) in the secondary market.
- Apple’s certain defense that its closed system improves performance and security. Suppose a court found no improvement and that their only function is to exclude rivals. Even the relatively conservative [Federal Circuit Court](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/us-federal-circuit/1281560.html) has acknowledged liability when the defendant redesigned its product simply to make generic complements incompatible.
- The government’s case will face substantial evidence that the iPhone system is indeed [superior](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1574013721000125?casa_token=U8JhAVfOXH0AAAAA:OCPhjWSefNRoXaNxflBN866dk1mPC9sKGbnCYXkBlCe4IsxhArJBc6ihIBIVwYF9hlJzRsn9NAA) in performance and [security](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/stamp/stamp.jsp?tp=&arnumber=9208339), and that its relatively closed ecosystem is critical to that.
