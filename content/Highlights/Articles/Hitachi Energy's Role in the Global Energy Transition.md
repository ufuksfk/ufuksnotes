## Highlights
- You might be surprised to hear that the new CEO of Hitachi Energy (appointed in July 2024), Andreas Schierenbeck, describes the company’s major challenge as keeping up with the growth in demand as increasingly economies and societies move to more and more electrification.
- "There is no question that renewables are the answer," said Schierenbeck, adding: "the limiting factors, wherever you are, are the political will, permitting, and finding compromises with industry and the public to make the necessary infrastructure build-out much faster."
