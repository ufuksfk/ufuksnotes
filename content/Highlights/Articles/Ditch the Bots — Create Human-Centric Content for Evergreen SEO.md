[[(SEO) Search Engine Optimization]]
## Highlights
- Most online content is written with the sole purpose of ranking high on Google, using techniques like keyword stuffing and rehashing competitors’ blog structures.
- If you have ever come across a lengthy blog that has disordered headings and lots of fluff information crammed in, it is precisely an attempt at the bland, conventional, and keyword-focused “SEO writing”.
- Even [[Google]] has noticed, taking steps to penalize low-quality content designed to manipulate rankings.
