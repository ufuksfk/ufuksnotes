## Highlights
- design is the rendering of intent, design is making decisions, design is Doing The Right Thing and The Right Thing To Do.
- The truth of the matter is that in most companies, this is accurate: an executive (or another stakeholder from “the business”) makes big decisions, and designers illustrate them.
- But there is a major difference: the developers are intending to be right, and the designers are intending to be wrong. This is because the developers have a very clear picture of what “right” looks like: the feature requirements. The definition of “value” has already been framed and the work is to deliver it to the customer.
