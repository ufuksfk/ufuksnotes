## Highlights
- **[My new book is now available in audio](https://sethgodin.supportingcast.fm/).** It’s not on Audible, at least not now. Audible has [exploited](https://pluralistic.net/2022/09/07/audible-exclusive/) their dominant position and the offer they make to authors is unfair and almost untenable.
- I think we’re about a year away from the majority of audiobooks being narrated by AI.
