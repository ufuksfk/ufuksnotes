- In my opinion, the hardest languages to master would be Basque first, then Hungarian, Estonian and Finnish, those languages not being based on the very probable Proto-Indo-European (PIE), of which almost all the other European languages descend.
- Basque is a language isolate, with no known parents aside the extinct Aquitanian. It’s a pre-Indo-European language with its very own lexicon, an important grammatical peculiarity, and you basically have to relearn everything from the ground.
- Hungarian, Finnish, and Estonian are Uralic languages, very different from the Indo-European languages, with a lot of cases and grammatical particularisms, but also with some influence from the neighboring languages (especially for Hungarian, from my very superficial knowledge of it).
