## Highlights
- The number of available consultants reached an **all-time high** (+26.9%).
- **Freelancing is growing fast**—over 80% of consultants on the registered consulting platforms are now independent professionals.
- Companies are looking for **flexibility**, meaning more short-term and project-based work.
- ![](https://media.licdn.com/dms/image/v2/D4E12AQHqrg1Hs6ANvQ/article-inline_image-shrink_1500_2232/B4EZT9Mj9JGgAg-/0/1739414717279?e=1747267200&v=beta&t=SeIPWLVKG2Vh9Jfa4sV3guCe3fNHVIdQpDxFbXM51A8)
- Companies are now **more selective** in hiring and prefer experts over generalists.
    - Note: bad news for me
- Consultant Rates Stay Low Despite Inflation
- Rates increased for **management, UX/UI, and data roles** but stagnated for **developers**.
- This suggests companies are willing to pay for expertise but are cost-conscious about generalist roles.
- Growth is highest in **major cities**, but **Västra Götaland, Gävleborg, and Kalmar** are emerging hotspots.
- Freelancing and short-term consulting contracts will keep increasing.
- Specialized skills will be more valuable than ever.
- **Tech & IT:** Strong demand for **data analysts, AI specialists, cybersecurity experts, and IT leaders**, but **general developer roles remain highly competitive** with lower wages.
- **Project Management & Business Strategy:** Companies are **investing in leadership roles**, so **project managers, change managers, and business analysts** will see steady demand.
