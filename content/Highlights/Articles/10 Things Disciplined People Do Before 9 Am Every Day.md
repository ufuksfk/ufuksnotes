## Highlights
- There are certain things disciplined people do before breakfast that truly set them apart. They know that by setting the tone in the morning, the rest of the day follows suit.
- This ritual of goal-setting is a powerful tool that keeps them motivated and productive throughout the day. There’s no better way to start the day than with a clear sense of purpose.
- I’ve noticed that disciplined people tend to prioritize physical activity. They understand that a healthy body contributes to a healthy mind and overall well-being.
- Disciplined people understand the importance of fueling their bodies with the right nutrients to start the day. Skipping breakfast is not an option for them.
- Disciplined people understand the power of the present moment. They make a conscious effort to start their day with mindfulness.
- This practice has been shown to reduce stress, improve focus, and promote overall mental well-being. It’s a way to connect with oneself before connecting with the world.
- Disciplined people have a unique approach to their to-do list. They tackle the most challenging tasks first thing in the morning.
- In an increasingly connected world, disciplined people understand the value of disconnecting. They resist the urge to check their emails or scroll through social media first thing in the morning.
- While the world is still quiet, disciplined individuals carve out time for restful activities. This could be a few extra minutes in bed, a leisurely walk, or simply sitting with a cup of coffee and watching the world wake up.
- This might seem like a waste of valuable time to some, but it’s actually a strategic move. By allowing themselves to ease into the day, they’re setting up their minds and bodies for optimal performance.
- Whether it’s reading a chapter of a book, listening to a podcast, or watching an educational video, they make sure to feed their minds with useful information.
- Disciplined people set their sights on success from the moment they wake up. They take time each morning to visualize their goals and the steps they need to take to achieve them.
