## Highlights
- Three user-definable control inputs [S0/S1/S2], for example, SSC selection, frequencyswitching, output enable, or power down
- The devices generate up to three output clocks from a singleinput frequency. Each output can be programmed in-system for any clock frequency up to 230 MHz, using theintegrated configurable PLL
