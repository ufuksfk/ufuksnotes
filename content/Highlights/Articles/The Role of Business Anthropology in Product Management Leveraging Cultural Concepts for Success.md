## Highlights
- [Business anthropology](https://www.mattartz.me/what-is-business-anthropology/) is the application of anthropological theories, methods, and practices to the business world. It involves using ethnographic research and cultural analysis to gain insights into the behavior of consumers, employees, and organizations in general. [Product management](https://www.mattartz.me/why-anthropologists-make-great-product-managers/) is the process of developing and marketing a product from inception to launch. In this blog, we will explore the role of business anthropology in product management and how leveraging cultural concepts can lead to success.
- One of the key benefits of business anthropology is its ability to help businesses understand local contexts. By conducting ethnographic research and analyzing cultural practices, business anthropologists can gain insights into the needs and desires of local communities. This knowledge can be used to develop products better suited to the local market.
- Business anthropologists can help businesses to gain a competitive advantage by linking anthropology to product development. By understanding the cultural and social factors that influence consumer behavior, business anthropologists can help businesses to develop products that are better suited to their target market.
