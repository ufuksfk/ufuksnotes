## Highlights
- Even if the methods of cybercriminals are becoming more and more perfidious, surrender to cyber threats is not a solution. Suppliers of medical devices, in particular, have a responsibility in this respect, which, in fact, is also required by law. Cyber risks and vulnerabilities that may affect internal IT, production or manufactured products must be continuously analyzed and remedied.
- In addition, companies should be aware that medical devices can already be manipulated in production. Operational technology (OT) environments in the medical field often consist of a variety of specialized devices connected to the network.
