## Highlights
- [[Spotify]] pays an average of $0.004 per stream.
- You probably had a time in your life where you listened to music until you fall asleep. Some users might be even doing it regularly every time they go to bed. Once you fall asleep, your music continues to play — draining your battery or in Spotify’s case, paying royalties for the songs that played.
- Assuming you sleep 7 hours, and an average song length of about 3 minutes, that is a total of $0.56 Spotify has to pay artists you’ve listened to that time. Not that bad for Spotify until you realized that they have an active user base of 381 million users as of Q3 2021.
- Now, not everybody is listening to music to sleep. Let’s assume 1% of their users do that at least once a month. In reality it might be less than that (or more), but let’s just think that the overall number looks like that when you consider people doing it more regularly or more than once a month. That is a little more than $2M dollars a month for Spotify.
- I think the sleep timer feature is a brilliant example of good product management. You introduced something that helps both the users and the company.
