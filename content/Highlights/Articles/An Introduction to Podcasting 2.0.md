## Highlights
- Many podcasters and podcast companies have been happy to allow large companies like [[Apple]] and [[Spotify]] to take the lead and define standards for the podcast industry.
- Adam Curry has been called the Father of Podcasting and has been involved in podcasting since its inception.
- The Podcast Index is an open repository that anyone can use to search for podcasts. It is, at this time, the most comprehensive list of podcasts available.
- <podcast:transcript> This tag is used to link to a transcript or closed captions file. Multiple tags can be present for multiple transcript formats.
