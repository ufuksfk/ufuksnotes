## Highlights
- A display is a **high bandwidth** output device. High bandwidth requires fast signals with high harmonic frequencies.
- The higher the frequencies, the more important the impedance of the return connection of the data signals becomes.
- Embedding a display is full of EMC risk
- Never trust a declaration of EMC conformity
- De-risk: perform pre-compliance measurements
