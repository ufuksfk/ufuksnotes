## Highlights
- Amazingly, the story blames Silicon Valley’s support of MAGA fascism entirely on the Democratic Party. The supposed reason: Democrats have in the past refused to give tech oligarchs everything they want. President Joe Biden resisted crypto and insisted on laws to protect against tech abuses. Multiple Democrats quoted in the piece say Democrats must do more to support “innovation”—code for capitulating to tech interests.
- But this framing fundamentally misrepresents what's actually happening in Silicon Valley today. As I said in my recent talk at the [Commonwealth Club of San Francisco](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z2W3tmCBEnE&t=3076s&ref=thenerdreich.com), Silicon Valley is currently about fascism, not innovation.
- The tech oligarchs, even the supposedly Democratic ones, apparently have nothing to say about the destruction of American democracy. They see only an opportunity to get more out of the system as Trump tears it apart.
- But bowing down to tech in 2025 means bowing down to Trump. Forget innovation—Silicon Valley has become a powerful force of collaboration with an authoritarian regime. Now, Democrats are being urged to accept this, to normalize it and to, in their own way, become collaborators as well.
- As the late David Golumbia wrote in [*Cyberlibertarianism: The Right Wing Politics of Digital Technology*](https://www.upress.umn.edu/9781517918149/cyberlibertarianism/?ref=thenerdreich.com):
- This is how cyberlibertarianism works: it is not so much about party politics as it is about controlling the terms of discourse and the conversation itself. This ensures that only rightist political formations ever get on the table and all concerns are subsumed under a general faith that digital technology will inevitably make things better, even if ‘better’ is never precisely defined.
