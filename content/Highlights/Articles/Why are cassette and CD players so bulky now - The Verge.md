## Highlights
- Cassette tape sales are at [a 20-year high](https://www.nme.com/news/music/cassette-tape-sales-at-20-year-high-3432784), the vinyl market is on a [17-year growth streak](https://www.theverge.com/2024/3/26/24112369/riaa-2023-music-revenue-streaming-vinyl-cds-physical-media), and CDs have [caught the attention of Gen Z](https://www.axios.com/2024/01/06/gen-z-cds-buying-collection). After a resurgence of retro physical media, tech companies have now jumped in to provide new hardware to play these formats.
