## Highlights
- **Joining Evterra**
  - Cody Oliver joined Evterra Recycling after talking to its CEO and realizing he wanted to contribute to their goal of becoming one of the largest electronics recyclers in the US. 
  - This led to new initiatives for plastics and the future of plastics recycling.

- **Electronics Plastic Content**
  - Plastics make up about 35% of electronic waste by weight.
  - It's crucial to ensure this e-waste ends up in the right recycling streams.
