## Highlights
- Since Vice President Harris cast the tie-breaking vote to pass the Inflation Reduction Act in 2022, [companies have invested $265 billion in clean energy, creating more than 330,000 new jobs](https://kamalaharris.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Policy_Book_Economic-Opportunity.pdf). National [battery storage](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=61202) is on track to double this year alone, and [solar module manufacturing capacity](https://seia.org/news/american-solar-panel-manufacturing-capacity-increases-71-q1-2024-industry-reaches-200-gigawatt/) is projected to be five times higher this year than when President Biden assumed office. Meanwhile, wind energy generation set new records in 2024, [exceeding coal generation for the first time in April](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=62784).
