[[History of Home Appliance]]
## Highlights
- Arguably though, a kitchen isn’t a kitchen if it isn’t filled with **appliances*****.***
- Today’s kitchens have changed from even just 20 years ago. It seems like now we have an appliance for everything – from deshelling hard-boiled eggs to mincing garlic. Kitchens are pretty high tech in the 21st century.
- ![](https://images.interestingengineering.com/img/iea/r1OrBmgROn/02-the-100-year-evolution-of-9-kitchen-appliances-refrigerator.png)
- These early ice boxes did the trick, but by around 1915, electric refrigerant style [[Refrigerator]] began showing up in homes around the world.
- However, these early devices, called Domeires used a variety of highly toxic gases as refrigerants. That meant that if your refrigerant stopped running, *you might too.*
- Nowadays, refrigerators use tetrafluoroethane to keep our food chilled.
- ![](https://images.interestingengineering.com/img/iea/r1OrBmgROn/03-the-100-year-evolution-of-9-kitchen-appliances-coffee-maker.png)
- The origins of coffee as a drink date back to the [[15th century]] in [[Ethiopia]] and Yemen.
- The first “machine” like appliance used for making coffee was the percolator originating in the 1860s. This technique of coffee making evolved until in the [[1920s]], vacuum system machines were introduced and coffee makers incorporated pumps and filtering systems.
- The groovy [[1970s]] brought about the invention of the [[Mr. Coffee]] branded coffee maker. While it may be today’s budget brand, Mr. Coffee machines were the first in-home drip brewing system for coffee. This invention helped to spread the popularity of coffee across industrialized populations.
- ![](https://images.interestingengineering.com/img/iea/r1OrBmgROn/04-the-100-year-evolution-of-9-kitchen-appliances-blender.png)
- In the [[1930s]], an appliance called the Miracle Mixer came along. This device iterated on the first invention of the blender and made it accessible to the public. [[Blender]]
- ![](https://images.interestingengineering.com/img/iea/r1OrBmgROn/05-the-100-year-evolution-of-9-kitchen-appliances-juicer.png)
- Today, most juicers use a twin-gear system that squeezes out the juice and keeps the nutrients intact.
- ![](https://images.interestingengineering.com/img/iea/r1OrBmgROn/06-the-100-year-evolution-of-9-kitchen-appliances-toaster.png)
- Ah, the simple toaster… how much could that have changed over the years? [[1909]] was the first year that a commercially successful toaster was sold, called the D-12 from GE. This toaster was one that surely would catch your house on fire as it was essentially just heated exposed coils that you would set your bread in to toast.
- Believe it or not, it wasn’t until Wonder Bread began selling pre-sliced bread loaves in the [[1930s]] that the toaster rose to prominence as being a breakfast-making machine, according to [Home Advisor.](https://www.homeadvisor.com/r/evolution-of-kitchen-appliances/)
- ![](https://images.interestingengineering.com/img/iea/r1OrBmgROn/07-the-100-year-evolution-of-9-kitchen-appliances-kettle.png)
- Believe it or not, by the [[1890s]], the world already had an electric kettle that could heat water by being plugged in.
- After this innovation, kettle designs were essentially just slowly improved upon as even the most advanced kettle tech today is still just an electric kettle.
- ![](https://images.interestingengineering.com/img/iea/r1OrBmgROn/08-the-100-year-evolution-of-9-kitchen-appliances-dishwasher.png)
- [[Dishwasher]] changed the world. First introduced in the 1890s as a hand-powered machine, the importance of these magical kitchen devices grew over the years. Permanent plumbing as part of home construction in the [[1920s]] is part of what caused the use of these machines to increase as more and more homes could be built with a place for a dishwasher.
- It really wasn’t until the [[1940s]] and [[1950s]] that dishwashers became the machine that we know and love with racks and spinning sprays. Other than new engineering tech, the core design really hasn’t changed much since then.
- ![](https://images.interestingengineering.com/img/iea/r1OrBmgROn/09-the-100-year-evolution-of-9-kitchen-appliances-stove.png)
- In 1900 the first gas-fueled stove was introduced.
- Gas would dominate the stove industry for nearly the next 3 decades until electric ovens were offered up as a safer and easier alternative.
- ![](https://images.interestingengineering.com/img/iea/r1OrBmgROn/10-the-100-year-evolution-of-9-kitchen-appliances-pressure-cooker.png)
- Pressure cooking was first experimented with in [[1679]] but really wouldn’t become a cooking method of popularity until the second World War. This was due to pressure cookers allowing for lower fuel usage, making them far cheaper to cook with.
- This appliance is one that really peaked in the mid-1900s and has been on the decline in use ever since. Nowadays we have appliances like the Instant pot that work to prepare out meals, but in general, we let our quick-cooking get done by the friendly [[microwave oven]]
