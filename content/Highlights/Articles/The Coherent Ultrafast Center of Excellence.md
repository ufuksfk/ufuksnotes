## Highlights
- we’ve built the **Ultrafast Center of Excellence** in Glasgow, Scotland—a state-of-the-art facility designed to fuel the future of ultrafast laser technology.
- At the heart of the Ultrafast Center of Excellence is the development and continuous improvement of high-performance laser systems like the new Chameleon Discovery LX,  the award-winning, cost-effective Axon, and the industry-leading Astrella. Stephen Duffy, Senior Director of Product Management at Coherent Scotland says, “These systems are designed with one goal in mind: to empower scientists and researchers to make significant breakthroughs in their respective fields.
- Whether there is a need for dual-output lasers for multiphoton microscopy, high-power fiber lasers for optogenetics, compact, economical OEM lasers for instrumentation, or kHz amplifiers for spectroscopy, the Ultrafast Center of Excellence manufactures superior products meeting all of these needs.
- We have achieved ISO14001 and ISO50001 certifications covering our Environmental and Energy Management Systems respectively.  As an illustration of our commitment to sustainability over the last three years, we reduced our electricity and gas consumption by 443,400kWh and 695,000kWh, respectively. In fiscal year 2024, the site was certified as Carbon Neutral  for Scope 1 and 2 emissions.
