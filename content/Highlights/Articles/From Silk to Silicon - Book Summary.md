## Highlights
- In the [[15th century]], a figure emerged in Portugal who would forever alter the course of global exploration — and, regrettably, initiate one of history's darkest chapters. Prince Henry of [[Portugal]], known to history as The Navigator, straddled the worlds of discovery and despair. From 1394 to 1460, his ceaseless quest for knowledge drove European ships to the edges of the known world and beyond, uncovering new territories and wealth.
- "From Silk to Silicon" by Jeffrey E. Garten. This compelling narrative unfolds the groundbreaking journeys of ten pivotal figures who have dramatically transformed the landscape of the global economy, linking distant corners of the world through their singular visions. From the strategic conquests of Genghis Khan to the digital revolutions spearheaded by Andrew Grove
- In the annals of history, few entities have wielded power and influence on the scale of the [[British East India Company]]. Known in its prime simply as The Company, its name was synonymous with an almost sovereign authority, rivaling the British government itself in wealth and power. Among the architects of its ascension, Sir Robert Clive stands out as a pivotal figure whose actions would forever change the landscape of the Indian subcontinent.
- The British East India Company, under Clive’s stewardship and beyond, evolved into an entity of extraordinary might. It levied taxes, commanded an army larger than those of many nations, and exerted control over major trade routes and ports. Its influence permeated every aspect of British and Indian economic life, generating immense profits for British shareholders while orchestrating the administration of an entire subcontinent.
- exploring the global financial empire forged by the British East India Company and unveiling how, despite their brute force or cunning economic strategies, these titans also paved the way for prosperity and progress.
- The paradoxical truth that even the most ruthless of conquerors and shrewd politicians have been catalysts for advancement and well-being;
- The ascension of [[China]], tracing its journey from a historical power to its resurgence as a formidable global force, challenging the United States for economic supremacy.
- In the intricate dance of global finance and communication, few figures stand as prominently as the Rothschild family and Cyrus Field. Their contributions, though arising from wholly different spheres—banking and telecommunication—left indelible marks on the world, reshaping how nations interact, finance develops, and information is exchanged across continents.
- [[Genghis Khan]] was laying the groundwork for unprecedented connectivity. He turned his attention to the Silk Road, the legendary trade route that spanned from East Asia to Europe, breathing new life into this artery of commerce and communication. This was not merely a pursuit of wealth; it was a strategic move to bind his vast territories together. By revitalizing the Silk Road, Khan facilitated a melting pot of cultures, innovations, and ideas across his empire.
