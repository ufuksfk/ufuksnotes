## Highlights
- but Matter is still far from being the easy-to-use, simple interoperability solution we were promised
- We’re also still missing some key features for devices (no energy management for smart plugs, adaptive lighting, or wide support of [PINs for door locks](https://www.theverge.com/2023/9/27/23891513/yale-assure-lock-2-touch-fingerprint-plus-homekey))
- Matter works locally over two wireless protocols, [Thread](https://www.theverge.com/23165855/thread-smart-home-protocol-matter-apple-google-interview) and Wi-Fi, with no required cloud dependency. This should make turning your smart lights on as fast as flipping a light switch (just more convenient). Matter also has [security built into the standard](https://zigbeealliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Matter-Security-Privacy_one-pager.pdf), so you can feel more confident about buying connected devices.
- Matter supports the following device types:
  • Light bulbs and light switches (including smart buttons)
  • Plugs and outlets
  • Locks
  • Thermostats and other HVAC controllers
  • Room air conditioners (i.e., window units)
  • Air purifiers
  • Fans
  • Blinds and shades
  • Robot vacuums
  • [[Refrigerator]] / Freezers
  • Washing machines
  • Dishwashers
  • [[Television]] and media devices
  • Smoke and CO alarms
  • Safety and security sensors
  • Air quality sensors (PM1, PM 2.5, PM 10, CO2, NO2, VOC, CO, ozone, radon, and formaldehyde)
  • Bridges
- It is also working on more features for existing device types, including energy management, water management features, and advanced lighting features.
