## Highlights
- She has worked as a design consultant to [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft), [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM), [Sony Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures), [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook), and [Pinterest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinterest). As of 2023 Kare has been an employee of [Niantic Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niantic_Labs).[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Kare#cite_note-sjm-2) As a pioneer of [pixel art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixel_art) and of the [graphical computer interface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_graphical_user_interface), she has been celebrated as one of the most significant designers of modern technology.
- ![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/29/Original_Mac_fonts.png)
- Mac typefaces designed c. 1983–84 by Susan Kare
- However, she had no experience in computer graphics and "didn't know the first thing about designing a typeface" or [pixel art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixel_art)[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Kare#cite_note-Iconic_Designer-14) so she drew heavily upon her fine art experience in [mosaics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosaic), [needlepoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Needlepoint), and [pointillism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointillism).
- He suggested that she get a US$2.50 grid notebook[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Kare#cite_note-How_Susan-5)[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Kare#cite_note-Celebrating-17) of the smallest graph paper she could find at the University Art store in Palo Alto[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Kare#cite_note-The_Sketchbook-18) and mock up several 32 × 32 pixel representations of his software commands and applications.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Kare#cite_note-Kare_AIGA-10) This includes an icon of scissors for the "[cut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cut,_copy,_and_paste)" command, a finger for "paste", and a paintbrush for [MacPaint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacPaint).[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Kare#cite_note-Macintosh_Smile-15)[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Kare#cite_note-Iconic_Designer-14)
