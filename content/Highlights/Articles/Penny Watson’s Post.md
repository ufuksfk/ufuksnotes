## Highlights
- In [[1990]], [[General Magic]] spun out of [[apple]] with an ambitious goal: to create a pocket-sized device that combined communication, computing, and information.
- Essentially, the first *smartphone* DECADES before its time.
- [[Andy Hertzfeld]] and [[Bill Atkinson]] (original Macintosh creators) 
  [[Tony Fadell]] father of the [[iPod]] and [[iPhone]]) 
  Megan Smith (future U.S. CTO) 
  [[Andy Rubin]] (creator of Android)
- 🤝 Big Partnerships: 
  General Magic partnered with giants like [AT&T](https://www.linkedin.com/company/att?trk=public_post-text), [[Organizations/SONY|SONY]] and [[Motorola]], aiming to bring its vision to life across a range of devices.
- To make matters worse, Apple then also launched the Newton MessagePad, which divided consumer attention and resources.
- When General Magic shut down in [[2002]], its alumni went on to create the iPhone, Android, Nest, and countless innovations that define tech today.
