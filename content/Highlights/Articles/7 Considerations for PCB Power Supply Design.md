## Highlights
- Some of the common issues to be handled in power supply design are, **EMI**, **trace design to handle high currents**, **reducing current loops**, **selection of components**, and **following the datasheets’ layout** **recommendations**.
- Before the advent of Switched-Mode Power Supplies (SMPS – switching power supplies) iron core transformers were used to convert high voltages 230VAC/110VAC to 12VAC.
    - Note: iron core on high voltage
