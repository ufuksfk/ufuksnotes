## Highlights
- In the [[17th century]], the statesman Axel Oxenstierna developed an administrative model for Sweden that, for centuries, was considered one of the world's most well-organized and successful bureaucracies. Oxenstierna laid the foundation for modern Sweden, with a centralized administration and neutral, professional civil servants whose loyalty lay with the state rather than with political power holders.
- A concrete example of this is how the leftist government in the city of [[Gothenburg]], since taking power after the 2022 election, has systematically violated both regulations and laws – to instead pursue an ideological policy that often directly conflicts with Sweden's government.
- With the same undemocratic disrespect, Gothenburg's leftist government also tried to make budget decisions in the city council instead of the municipal assembly – which, in short, meant deviating from the existing budget practice to try to force their will through – despite being in the minority.
- As if it were not undemocratic enough for a municipality to try to opt out of laws it dislikes, Gothenburg's leftist government also decided, in direct violation of municipal law, to boycott all goods from Israel.
