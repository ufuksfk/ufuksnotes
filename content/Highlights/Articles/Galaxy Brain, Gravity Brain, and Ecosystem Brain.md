## Highlights
- Galaxy Brain people are often designers, prototypers, and art directors. Their love language is deep work and exploration.
    - Note: mine is not that
- Many Gravity Brains become managers of people (usually first line) or programs or product managers. Their love language is clarity, specificity, and sequencing.
    - Note: Maybe I am more gravity brain?
- Many Ecosystem Brains are researchers, managers, and systems designers. Their love language is connecting the dots and architecting.
    - Note: this is me as well
