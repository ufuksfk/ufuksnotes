## Highlights
- The company was formerly known as Hitachi ABB Power Grids, following a joint venture with Hitachi Ltd. and [ABB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABB) Power Grids in 2020, and was rebranded in 2021 as Hitachi Energy Ltd.
- In July 2020, [ABB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABB) divested its power grid business and sold 80.1% of that to [Hitachi Ltd.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi), leading to the formation of **Hitachi ABB Power Grids**.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi_Energy#cite_note-1)
- In October 2021, Hitachi ABB Power Grids changed its company name to Hitachi Energy. In December 2022, it was confirmed that [Hitachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi) acquired the remaining 19.9 percent shares from [ABB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABB) Ltd.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi_Energy#cite_note-2)
- Hitachi Energy offers [high-voltage switchgear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-voltage_switchgear) and breakers, [high-voltage direct current](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-voltage_direct_current), [transformers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformer), [substations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substation), and [power-system automation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power-system_automation).
- Hitachi Energy has been involved in several notable projects, such as:
- Supplier for Changji-Guquan UHVDC [transmission line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transmission_line) in China, the world’s first transmission line operating at 1,100 kV voltage (2018)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi_Energy#cite_note-3)
- Fluoronitrile-based gas replacement for the greenhouse gas [sulphur hexafluoride](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulfur_hexafluoride) for National Grid in Kent, UK (2021)[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi_Energy#cite_note-4)
- HVDC converter stations for [high-voltage direct current](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-voltage_direct_current) [submarine power cable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submarine_power_cable) [North Sea Link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Sea_Link) (2021)[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi_Energy#cite_note-5)
- [Renewable energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renewable_energy) [microgrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microgrid) for the remote community of [Gull Bay First Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gull_Bay_First_Nation) in Canada (2022)[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi_Energy#cite_note-6)
- Offshore platforms and onshore HVDC converter stations for electricity grid operator [TenneT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TenneT)[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi_Energy#cite_note-7)
