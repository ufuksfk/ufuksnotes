[[Mercantilism]]
## Highlights
-  [[Seaport at sunset]], a French seaport painted by [[Claude Lorrain]] in [[1639]], at the height of mercantilism
  **Mercantilism** is an [economic policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_policy) that is designed to maximize the exports and minimize the imports for an economy. It promotes [imperialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperialism), [colonialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonialism), [tariffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tariffs) and [subsidies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsidies) on traded goods to achieve that
