## Highlights
- These include basic [insulation](https://www.raypcb.com/pcb-insulation/), functional insulation, altitude, creepage distance, reinforced insulation, double insulation, supplementary insulation, conductive elements, and clearance.
    - Tags: [[high voltage pcb]] 
- With respect to the spacing of the electrical conductor, boards that have a high voltage difference of 80V between the conductors must have these qualities between them.
  • There should be 0.1 mm between the internal layers
  • The uncoated external layers must have [0.6 mm](https://www.raypcb.com/0-6mm-pcb/) in-between them
  • Also, the uncoated external layers must have about 0.6mm in-between them
  • Between the coated external layers, there should be 0.13mm in-between them
  Note that these values are the minimum that these layers ought to have between them.
    - Note: thickness between layers
