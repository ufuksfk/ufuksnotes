## Highlights
- [[(JIT) Just-in-Time Production]] is only one element of lean manufacturing, which is a broader philosophy that seeks to eliminate all kinds of waste in a process.
- In post-war Japan, Taiichi Ohno (“Father of JIT”) adapted the system at [[Toyota]] to handle smaller batch sizes and more variety in the parts that could be used to construct assemblies.
- In [[1952]], work on their JIT system was initiated, with full deployment of the *kanban* pull system by [[1962]].
