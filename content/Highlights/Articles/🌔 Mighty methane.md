## Highlights
- To understand its mighty power, consider that one ton of methane in the atmosphere is equivalent to over [80 tons of carbon dioxide](https://p.feedblitz.com/t3/1124890/235515391/14374517%5F/thecarbonalmanac.org/370?utm%5Fsource=feedblitz&utm%5Fmedium=email&utm%5Fcampaign=DailyDifference&utm%5Fcontent=Mighty%20methane) in the first twenty years after emission.
- The good news is that there is a new tool launching that will help track methane emissions. Using satellites, [MethaneSAT](https://p.feedblitz.com/t3/1124890/235515391/14374517%5F/~www.methanesat.org/satellite/?utm%5Fsource=feedblitz&utm%5Fmedium=email&utm%5Fcampaign=DailyDifference&utm%5Fcontent=Mighty%20methane) will be able to help identify sources of methane, both small and large, with accurate location information.
