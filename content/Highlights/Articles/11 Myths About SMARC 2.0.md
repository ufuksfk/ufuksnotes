## Highlights
- When looking at the 84- x 55-mm COM Express Mini, the form factor delivers comparable performance to the 82- x 50-mm-sized SMARC 2.0.
    - Tags: [[smarc]] 
- SMARC 2.0 has 314 pins; more interfaces than COM Express Mini. So, only those applications with interface requirements that exceed the capabilities of SMARC 2.0 need consider PICMG’s COM Express specification.
- Qseven is well-established in the embedded market and has a broad range of supporters, ensuring that Qseven will remain the better design candidate for deeply embedded and cost-effective embedded-system designs.
