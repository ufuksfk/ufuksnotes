- **Tech's Focus on Profits**
  - Tech companies were always focused on maximizing shareholder value, contrary to the pretense of altruistic motives. 
  - The instant wealth from early internet stocks further solidified this profit-driven approach, overshadowing the initial vision of spreading knowledge.
