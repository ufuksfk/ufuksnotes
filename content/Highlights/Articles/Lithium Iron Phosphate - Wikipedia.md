## Highlights
- LFP cells have an operating voltage of 3.3 V, [charge density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charge_density) of 170 mAh/g, high [power density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_density), long cycle life and stability at high temperatures.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate#cite_note-SciData8(2024)165-18)
- LFP's major commercial advantages are that it poses few safety concerns such as overheating and explosion, as well as long cycle lifetimes, high power density and has a wider operating temperature range. Power plants and automobiles use LFP.[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate#cite_note-19)[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate#cite_note-20)
- The safety is a crucial property for certain applications. For example, in 2016 an LFP-based energy storage system was installed in [Paiyun Lodge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paiyun_Lodge) on [Mt.Jade (Yushan)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yu_Shan) (the highest alpine lodge in [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan)). As of 2024, the system is still operating safely.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate#cite_note-Batteries10(2024)202-3)
- Although LFP has 25% less specific energy (Wh/g) than lithium batteries with [oxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxide) (e.g. nickel-cobalt-manganese, NCM) cathode materials, primarily due to its operational voltage (3.2 volts vs 3.7 for NCM-type cathode chemistries), it has 70% more than [nickel-hydrogen batteries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nickel%E2%80%93hydrogen_battery).
- The major differences between LFP batteries and other lithium-ion battery types is that LFP batteries contain no [cobalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cobalt) (removing ethical and economic questions about cobalt's availability) and have a flat discharge curve.
- LFP batteries have drawbacks, originating from a high electronic [resistivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resistivity) of LFP, as well as the lower maximum charge/discharge voltage. The [energy density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_density) is significantly lower than LiCoO 
  2 (although higher than the [nickel–metal hydride battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nickel%E2%80%93metal_hydride_battery)).
