## Highlights
- here are more than 97,000 health and fitness apps available for mobile and tablet devices. Over half (52%) of smartphone users get health-associated information from their device and 15% of 18-29 year olds have health apps installed.
- According to leading market research company Technavio, the fitness app market has the potential to grow by $1.68 billion in 2020-2024.
- These types of apps should try to vary the methods of exercise to keep users motivated and engaged.
