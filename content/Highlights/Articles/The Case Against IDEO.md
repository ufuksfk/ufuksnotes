## Highlights
- Over the years, large design consultancies have built a reputation for delivering cutting-edge insights and groundbreaking innovations. Their pitch is simple: pay us a large sum of money, and we’ll do the research, design, and thinking for you. It’s a compelling promise, one that has fueled the growth of multi-million-dollar design firms like [IDEO](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ideo?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-mention) . But as businesses face increasing pressure to move faster and more efficiently, it’s time to ask: do these agencies actually deliver on their promise, or are they simply protecting an outdated business model?
- We found that many companies spent six to seven figures on consultancy fees, only to walk away with elaborate slide decks and vague design principles rather than actionable solutions. Some projects took months—if not years—to complete, with deliverables that felt generic, repetitive, and overly academic.
- Additionally, the increasing adoption of AI-driven research tools is reshaping the landscape, making traditional firms less relevant. AI can analyze vast datasets, simulate user behaviors, and produce iterative results in real time—something traditional consultancies struggle to match without excessive human capital.
- Companies are frequently told things like “Users want a more seamless experience” or “Customers appreciate intuitive design” after months of expensive research.
- Large consultancies aren’t known for their speed. Their processes—while carefully curated to justify high price tags—are slow, layered with bureaucracy, and often involve too many stakeholders
