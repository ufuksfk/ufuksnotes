## Highlights
- **Routing**. I never really figured out the URL setup for blog posts in the Academic theme for Hugo; and I have a number of old posts from a Hakyll setup I breifly explored in 2015-2017 before abandoning it
- **Image Components**. This is a big one for me. For instance, I want to have [a gallery](https://benschmidt.org/gallery) where I can just show visuals that will show a tile of images. And since it’s the 2020s, that needs to look one way on desktop and quite a different way on mobile.
- **CSS and themes**. CSS is incredibly powerful, and incredibly hard to use with most frameworks I’ve explored. One reason is that the CSS gets shunted off into some file somewhere called ‘ /lib/app.scss ’ or something and it’s never clear from the css which things are boilerplate, which are essential classes used everywhere, and which are not used on a site at all.
- Blog posts have got to stay in Markdown. I just chose to shove most of the contents of the Hugo tree into ‘ src/content ’
