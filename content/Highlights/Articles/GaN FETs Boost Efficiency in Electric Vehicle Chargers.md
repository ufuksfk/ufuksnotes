## Highlights
- THE [[PHASE-SHIFT FULL BRIDGE (PSFB)]] is a classic topology for applications that must accommodate a wide range of operating voltages, as with battery chargers. A PSFB converter generally uses four power switches ([[MOSFET]]s or [[IGBT]]s) to form a full bridge on the primary side of an isolation transformer.
