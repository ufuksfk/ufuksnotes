## Highlights
- *Teresa Amabile, a Baker Foundation Professor at Harvard Business School and the author of* [Growing Up Creative](https://characterlab.us3.list-manage.com/track/click?u=1f06e5697fd8484482393636c&id=d8edec0fb7&e=939df651dd) *and* [The Progress Principle](https://characterlab.us3.list-manage.com/track/click?u=1f06e5697fd8484482393636c&id=311519a1c4&e=939df651dd) *, talked to us about how to foster* [*creativity*](https://characterlab.us3.list-manage.com/track/click?u=1f06e5697fd8484482393636c&id=83379f35b5&e=939df651dd) *. Here are a few highlights:*
    - Tags: [[person]] 
- Feeling that you’re being watched while you’re working. Feeling that you’re competing with other people. Working for reward—feeling that you’re doing something to get money or a grade—can decrease intrinsic interest in doing something.
    - Note: what makes you less creative
