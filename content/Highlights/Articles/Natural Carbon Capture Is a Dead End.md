## Highlights
- Blue carbon specifically refers to the carbon stored in specific marine environments, namely seagrass, mangrove forests, salt marshes, and peat
- Small changes in sea levels, rising temperatures, and changes in methane and nitrous oxide levels can collapse these ecosystems and release all the carbon stored in them.
