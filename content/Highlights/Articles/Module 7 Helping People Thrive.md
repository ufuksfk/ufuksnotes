## Highlights
- Too often, technologists' visions of a better future miss kindness outright. Instead, they prioritize values like convenience, efficiency, or novelty.
- Other examples of using technology to promote kindness include:
  • Quora’s implementation of a “Thanks” button for when someone answers your question.
    - Note: how tech promote kindness
- Facebook’s Memorialized Accounts for loved ones who have passed away (shown below) [3-17].
    - Tags: [[facebook]] 
- Some of the top five regrets of the dying include:
  • *I wish I'd had the courage to live a life true to myself, not the life others expected of me.*
  • *I wish I hadn't worked so hard.*
  • *I wish I'd had the courage to express my feelings.*
  • *I wish I had stayed in touch with my friends.*
  • *I wish that I had let myself be happier* [3-19].
