## Highlights
- **OKI Circuit Technology** of [[Japan]] has officially introduced a **124-layer PCB**, marking the highest known **commercial stack** height for semiconductor test boards to date. (The former record holder was 108 layers.) Even more impressive, they achieved this without exceeding the standard **7.6 mm board thickness** (just under ⅓"), which has long been a mechanical and thermal constraint in wafer-level test systems.
- On paper, jumping from 108 to 124 layers may not sound earth-shattering. But in practice, that’s a **15% increase in routing density**—achieved without compromising board thickness, reliability, or signal integrity.
- **Ultra-thin dielectric layers** just 25 µm thick
- According to *All About Circuits*, these boards:
  • Cost approximately **$4,800 per square meter**
  • Require **up to 16 weeks** to produce
  • Deliver **yield rates around 65%** (compared to 85% for 108-layer builds)
