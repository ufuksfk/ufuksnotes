## Highlights
- Approximately 5,300 years ago, the populations of [[Scandinavia]] and northwestern Europe faced a significant collapse. Settlements were abandoned, and farming communities disappeared. The cause of this demographic shift has puzzled scholars, with theories ranging from agricultural crises to conflicts. However, recent genetic research published in *Nature[1](https://www.anthropology.net/p/new-genetic-research-explores-the/#footnote-1-150956063)* offers another possibility: the ancient plague bacterium *Yersinia pestis*.
