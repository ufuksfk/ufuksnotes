## Highlights
- Don’t re-post the content immediately after the original article is published. As suggested by the [HubSpot Content Marketing Certification](https://www.impactplus.com/blog/guide-to-hubspot-certifications) course, you should wait at least two weeks for that article to be [indexed by Google](https://www.impactplus.com/blog/how-long-does-it-take-google-to-index-a-new-page). If both pieces of content are published at the same time, it can be difficult for Google to distinguish which version they should include/exclude in their index.

    - Note: wait 2 weeks for the original content and repost it
