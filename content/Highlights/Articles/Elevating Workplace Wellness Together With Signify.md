## Highlights
- At the forefront of this shift, Secto Design has joined forces with Signify, the world leader in lighting solutions best known for its Philips brand, to bring the benefits of Human Centric Lighting and premium design to the workplace.
- Daylight helps us align our circadian rhythm or sleep-wake -cycle, which is a key factor in our overall well-being both at and outside work. Indoors, the light level intensity is generally tuned according to the visual functions of light, but the biological impact is often overlooked. Human Centric Lighting (HCL) aims to satisfy not only our visual demands but also our biological and emotional needs.
- The Philips BioUP technology developed by Signify is a lighting solution designed to bring the benefits of daylight indoors. Enhancing the LED light spectrum with cyan light, it increases the biological impact of light to balance the natural rhythm of our bodies–without changing the visual colour or intensity of the light.
