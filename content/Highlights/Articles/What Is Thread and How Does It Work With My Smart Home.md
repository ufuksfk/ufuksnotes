## Highlights
- Thread is a mesh networking technology that connects your small smart devices—from motion sensors to door locks—and offers a better, more efficient way for them to work together. Thread comes built into smart devices. To use it, you'll need a Thread border router to connect the thread network to your home Wi-Fi. Once that's in place, any Thread-compatible device—up to 250 at a time—can connect, regardless of the manufacturer.
    - Note: what is thread?
- Some types of Thread border routers include:
  • Apple® HomePod® Mini
  • Apple TV® 4K (2nd gen)
  • Eero® Wi-Fi routers (Wi-Fi 5 and newer)
  • Google Nest™ Wi-Fi points and routers
  • Google Nest Hub Max™
  • Google Nest Hub (2nd gen)
  • Nanoleaf® Shapes, Lines, Elements

- With Thread, your smart home will be, well, smarter. Your devices will work faster and more reliably across a larger area, and they'll use less power than they would on Wi-Fi. Since Thread connects smart devices to each other, the network can also continue working, glitch-free, even if service is spotty, your internet goes down, or a device fails.
    - Note: advantages of thread
- Matter™ is a new standard for communication that simplifies your smart home so your devices can talk to each other regardless of the manufacturer.
