[[Tribalism]]: The division of society into insular groups, often reinforced by shared beliefs, identity, or ideology.

**High IQ vs. [[Misinformation]]**: Intelligence does not necessarily protect against misinformation; in some cases, it can make people more skilled at rationalizing falsehoods.

Just as [[Netflix]] disrupted traditional mass media, small echo chambers in [[social media]] function similarly to political parties or think tanks—reinforcing specific narratives.

**Different Perspectives on Tribalism**:
- **Sociologists** describe it as _homophily_ (the tendency to associate with similar people).
- **Political theorists** call it _identity politics_.
- **Writers** refer to it as _retribalization_ (a return to smaller, more fragmented social groups).

**Examples of [[Tribalism]]**:
- People may emphasize their identity (e.g., being Black, a woman, etc.) and highlight statistics on societal disadvantages.
- While this reaction is normal and understandable, it can ultimately harm democratic discourse by deepening divisions rather than fostering solutions.
